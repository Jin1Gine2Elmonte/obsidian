# Cosmology & Concept Atlas

## Existence Sublime / الوجود الأبهى
A major cosmological anchor. It belongs to the highest layer of the user's fictional metaphysics and should be treated as a node with relationships to Jin, the Entity of Forgetting, Primordial Rift, and Shadows of the First Creation.

## Entity of Forgetting / كيان النسيان
The central paradox: absolute nothingness cannot manifest as a literal entity, because manifestation itself creates existence. The Entity of Forgetting therefore belongs to existence while functioning around the concept of forgetting. This allows the story to explore the boundary between being, absence, memory, identity, and erasure.

## Primordial Rift
A foundational cosmological rupture. It should be treated as an origin-layer event rather than merely a location or magical phenomenon unless future canon specifies otherwise.

## Shadows of the First Creation / ظلال الخلق الأول
A major mythological framework and title anchor. The phrase implies that the present cosmos may be affected by remnants, consequences, echoes, or residues of an earlier creation event. Future canon should determine whether these shadows are literal entities, historical traces, metaphysical laws, memories, or something more fundamental.

## Abyss
A recurring deep-cosmology anchor associated with mystery, darkness, and extreme metaphysical distance.

## Corruption
A recurring force/concept. It has appeared in a post-nuclear/corrupted-world framing and may represent transformation, decay, contamination, or metaphysical distortion depending on the project.

## Curse of the Ancients
A recurring historical/cosmological anchor associated with inherited consequences from an earlier age.

## Blood family
A recurring lineage anchor. It connects the power system, inheritance, family politics, identity, and historical consequences.

## Dreams of the Inverted Universe
A recurring conceptual/title anchor suggesting a reality whose structure, perspective, causality, or ontology is inverted relative to ordinary experience.

## Imagination realm
A major premise: imagination can operate as a place that does not physically exist in ordinary reality while nevertheless influencing human beings. This creates a bridge between psychology, fiction, metaphysics, and narrative reality.

## Meta-fictional reality
Jin's fictional awareness creates a layer where characterhood, narration, reality, and authorship can become part of the same system. This should be treated as a structural property of the universe rather than a simple fourth-wall joke.

## Nothingness versus function
A recurring philosophical distinction:
- absolute nothingness: no entity, no agency, no manifestation
- existent function/entity: something that exists and can perform an operation involving absence, loss, erasure, or forgetting

The distinction is foundational to the Entity of Forgetting concept.

## Cosmological writing rule
High-level concepts should have consequences at lower levels. A cosmic rule should eventually affect character choices, institutions, language, history, powers, geography, or perception; otherwise it risks becoming decorative lore.
