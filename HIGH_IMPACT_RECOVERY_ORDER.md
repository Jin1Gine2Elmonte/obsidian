# High-Impact Recovery Order

## Purpose
Prioritize recovery work by how much a recovered source can change the meaning of the existing archive. This is a recovery queue, not a source of new canon.

## Tier 0 — identity / continuity breakers

### 1. Jin ↔ Ryota identity
Why first: resolving or separating these identities can change the child-history branch, Almont relationship, Arisa references, chronology, project membership, and Jin-version lattice.

Best evidence:
- direct user statement equating or separating the names;
- a scene where both names appear;
- explicit version/rewrite language;
- distinct ages, events, or relationships.

### 2. Jin variant genealogy
Why second: the meaning of later Jin statements depends on which Jin they describe.

Best evidence:
- named variants;
- replacement/rewrite statements;
- project/version markers;
- explicit continuity or discontinuity.

## Tier 1 — foundational character events

### 3. Arisa death scene
Potentially constrains:
- witness identity;
- Almont response;
- Ryota/Jin chronology;
- later rebellion/escape;
- family emotional architecture.

### 4. Almont ↔ son dialogue / motive scenes
Recover actual dialogue before inferring ideology, affection, fear, succession intent, or psychological causes.

### 5. Adam's first meeting with Jin
Potentially establishes:
- why Adam follows;
- which Jin he follows;
- whether he recognizes variants;
- the first world-travel context;
- independence versus dependence.

### 6. Defining Garthin scenes
Recover actions that demonstrate the contradiction rather than expanding adjectives or moral labels.

## Tier 2 — system rules

### 7. Power-system mechanics
Recover exact rules for Magic, inherited Powers, spiritual vessel, limits, costs, inheritance, and interaction.

### 8. Imperial machinery
Recover the actual city hierarchy, military structure, communication, resources, succession, and Dark Forest policy only where explicitly designed.

### 9. Dark Forest history
Recover why it lies outside effective imperial control and what this boundary means in canon.

## Tier 3 — cosmological structure

### 10. Cosmological relationship evidence
Recover explicit links among Existence Sublime, Entity of Forgetting, Primordial Rift, Shadows of the First Creation, Corruption, Abyss, and related anchors.

### 11. Entity of Forgetting source scenes
The philosophical distinction is strong, but scenes can establish whether the preserved paradox belongs to one project generation or multiple uses of the concept.

## Tier 4 — project-specific recovery

### 12. Strings of Fate story transcripts
The visual memory is strong. Narrative recovery should target:
- protagonist/cast;
- plot events;
- world rules;
- string mechanics;
- confirmed themes;
- revisions/rejected directions;
- relationships;
- ending.

Do not infer story mechanics from the title.

## Recovery execution rule
For each recovered source:
1. preserve raw evidence;
2. identify project/version;
3. classify the claim;
4. update its canonical owner;
5. propagate verified deltas;
6. record knowledge-state transition;
7. update conflicts and supersession;
8. only then revise summaries.

## Anti-hallucination rule
A lower-priority recovery item must not be creatively expanded merely because a higher-priority source is unavailable. Missing evidence remains missing evidence.

## Completion metric
Recovery is valuable when one source resolves multiple high-impact uncertainties or creates a verified relationship between major nodes.
