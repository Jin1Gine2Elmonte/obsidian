# Project Atlas

## Obsidian Memory
Current repository: Jin1Gine2Elmonte/obsidian. Purpose: durable external memory and knowledge substrate. The user wants it to become a serious portable archive rather than a simple notes folder. Obsidian is intended to remain the primary source of truth, with MCP/API/bridge layers providing AI access.

## Nexus
Family of agent/server experiments. Common architecture: Node.js, Google GenAI SDK, MCP, memory, tools, orchestration, remote deployment. Treat different repositories/iterations as distinct generations unless explicitly linked.

## Hermes
Agent/deployment project involving Hermes CLI/YAML, Docker, Northflank, knowledge storage, embeddings, and GitHub Actions. Files and deployment artifacts have included Dockerfile, hermes.yaml, northflank.yaml, docker-compose.yml, deploy.sh, README_ARABIC.md, NORTHFLANK_SETUP.md, knowledge_os.py, and workflow configuration.

## Novel universe
Large dark fantasy/cosmic mythology centered on Jin/Gin/Gene, Adam, Garthin, Almont, Arisa, Benjamin, Ryota, the empire, Dark Forest, Entity of Forgetting, Existence Sublime, Primordial Rift, and Shadows of the First Creation.

## Whispers of Fog
Medieval-fantasy side novel; 20-chapter structure explored.

## Strings of Fate
Cover-art project with translucent white glowing strings carrying a subtle light-emerald tint. Existing composition, city, character, lighting, colors, title, dimensions, and sky should remain intact; unwanted bottom text should be removed and decoration under the title improved.

## The Last Thing to Be Forgotten
Orchestral-operatic-cosmic work about the Entity of Forgetting. The philosophical distinction between nothingness and an existent entity/function of forgetting is foundational.

## Shadows of the First Creation
Major mythological/cosmological title and anchor concept with cover-art development.

## Lord of the Abyss
Recurring novel/title concept in the dark mythological setting.

## Dark novel website
Explored dark novel publishing/site concepts including Noir-novels.com, WordPress, and Netlify.

## RPG / interactive fiction
Explored HTML RPG interfaces and progression mechanics inspired by progression fantasy systems such as Solo Leveling/Nano Machine, while developing an original system appropriate to the user's world.

## AI memory portability
A continuing cross-model project: preserve context in a model-independent format so ChatGPT, Gemini, Qwen, and other systems can use the same durable knowledge. Conversation export, normalization, semantic retrieval, MCP, and Android interaction layers have all been explored.
