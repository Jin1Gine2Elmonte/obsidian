# Negative Knowledge Registry

## Purpose
Record important things the archive has learned **not** to assume.

Negative knowledge is valuable because future models tend to regenerate plausible but previously rejected interpretations.

## Identity
- Do not assume every Jin/Gin/Gene is one entity.
- Do not assume Jin and Ryota are the same character.
- Do not assume a similar biography proves identity.
- Do not assume later cosmic abilities belong to earlier versions.

## Project boundaries
- Do not assume every dark/cosmic title belongs to the same universe.
- Do not merge Strings of Fate with the Jin cosmology without source evidence.
- Do not merge Entity of Forgetting with the AI memory system; their adjacency is conceptual, not literal.
- Do not infer that thematic reuse means canonical continuity.

## Worldbuilding
- Do not reduce the empire's declared scale to realistic medieval scale simply to make logistics easier.
- Do not turn derived institutional consequences into remembered canon.
- Do not assume the Dark Forest is evil merely because it is dangerous and mysterious.
- Do not assume the Blood family is identical to Almont's dynasty.

## Character interpretation
- Do not transform Garthin's hidden kindness into a redemption arc.
- Do not infer Arisa's personality from the fact that she dies.
- Do not infer Almont's private motives from his public tyranny alone.
- Do not reduce Adam to a passive follower without recovering independent decisions.

## Power system
- Do not invent exact mechanics for Magic, inherited Powers, or the spiritual vessel from generic fantasy conventions.
- Do not assign limits/costs that are not sourced.
- Do not treat extraordinary reality control as proof that every Jin has identical capabilities.

## Cosmology
- Do not build a complete hierarchy from the names Existence Sublime, Primordial Rift, Shadows of the First Creation, Abyss, Corruption, or Curse of the Ancients alone.
- Do not interpret Primordial Rift literally as a physical crack without evidence.
- Do not interpret Entity of Forgetting as absolute nothingness.
- Do not interpret Shadows of the First Creation as a specific type of entity until source material establishes it.

## Strings of Fate
- Do not invent red-string mythology because of the title.
- Do not assume deterministic fate.
- Do not assume time travel.
- Do not assume a fate deity.
- Do not assume the strings can be cut, touched, seen, or manipulated.
- Do not turn visual cover requirements into plot rules.

## Archive behavior
- Do not equate more words with more knowledge.
- Do not create a new file when an existing node should be corrected or expanded.
- Do not erase historical contradictions merely to create a cleaner summary.
- Do not let assistant synthesis outrank direct user evidence.
- Do not hide uncertainty behind confident prose.
- Do not treat an analytical question as an established fact.
- Do not replace a missing transcript with a fabricated reconstruction.

## Core principle
The archive should remember not only what became true, but which interpretations became unsafe, obsolete, rejected, superseded, or unsupported.
