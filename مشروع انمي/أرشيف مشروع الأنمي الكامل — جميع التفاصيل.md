# أرشيف الغياب — العالم والقصة والموسم

## هوية العمل

- **العنوان العربي:** أرشيف الغياب
- **العنوان الإنجليزي:** THE ARCHIVE OF ABSENCE
- **النوع:** فانتازيا مظلمة، غموض، إثارة نفسية، دراما، أكشن خارق.
- **الموسم الأول:** 12 حلقة.
- **الحلقة الأولى:** صوت لم يُسجّل.
- **مدة الحلقة الأولى:** 10 دقائق، مقسمة إلى 75 مقطعًا، مدة كل مقطع 8 ثوانٍ.

## الفكرة المحورية

> ماذا لو لم يكن النسيان فقدانًا للذاكرة، بل عملية تُبقي شيئًا مرعبًا على قيد الحياة؟

العالم لا يعامل الذكريات بوصفها معلومات داخل العقل فقط؛ فالذكريات المقتطعة يمكن أن تبقى في صورة مادية، وأن تتراكم خارج أصحابها، وأن تكتسب في بعض الحالات قدرة على التفكير والمراقبة والبحث عن أصحابها الأصليين.

## العالم: نوراث

تقع الأحداث في مدينة تُدعى **نوراث**، مدينة ضخمة لا تعرف شروق الشمس. فوقها قمر أسود هائل، وفي أعماقها مؤسسة مرعبة تُسمى **أرشيف الغياب**.

يستطيع سكان نوراث التخلص من ذكرياتهم المؤلمة عبر تسليمها إلى الأرشيف. تُسحب الذكرى من عقل صاحبها بإبرة فضية، ثم تُحفظ داخل قارورة زجاجية صغيرة، وكأنها شيء مادي يمكن تخزينه.

لكن الذكريات التي تُنتزع لا تختفي. إنها تتراكم في طبقة مظلمة تحت المدينة، وهناك تتحول بعض الذكريات إلى كائنات تستطيع التفكير والمراقبة والبحث عن أصحابها الأصليين.

يسميها الأرشيف: **المنسيّون**.

## القواعد الأساسية للقوى

### الأثر

لكل إنسان ظلّ ذاكرة يتشكل من أقوى ذكرياته. قد يتغير شكله تبعًا لما يخفيه صاحبه.

### الاقتطاع

يمكن انتزاع ذكرى من الإنسان وحفظها، لكن المشاعر المرتبطة بها لا تختفي تمامًا.

### الاسترجاع الصامت

قدرة نادرة تسمح لصاحبها بسماع ذكريات الأشياء ولمس أحداث الماضي من خلالها. كل استرجاع ينتزع منه ذكرى صغيرة من حاضره.

### المنسيّون

تجليات للذكريات المقتطعة، تختلف أشكالهم ووعيهم باختلاف طبيعة الذكريات التي تكوّنوا منها.

## القصة الرئيسية

**إيلان سار**، شاب في التاسعة عشرة، يعمل متدربًا في أرشيف الغياب. يبدو هادئًا، قليل الكلام، ولا يثق كثيرًا بما يتذكره.

منذ طفولته، يطارده شعور غريب بأنه كان يملك أختًا صغيرة.

لا يتذكر وجهها. لا يعرف اسمها. لا توجد صورة لها في منزله، ولا سجل عائلي يثبت وجودها. حتى والده ينكر ذلك.

لكن إيلان لم يتخلص من إحساسه بها قط.

وذات ليلة، يعثر على طائر خشبي صغير داخل أحد صناديق الأرشيف المهجورة. عندما يلمسه، يسمع صوت فتاة تناديه باسمه.

الصوت لا يأتي من ذاكرته.

بل من الطائر نفسه.

ومنذ تلك اللحظة، يبدأ شيء ما في أعماق المدينة بالاستيقاظ.

## الشخصيات الأساسية

### إيلان سار — Eilan Sar

شاب في التاسعة عشرة. متدرب في الأرشيف. شعر أسود تتخلله خصلة فضية قرب الصدغ الأيسر، عينان رماديتان، ومعطف كحلي داكن بأزرار نحاسية، وخيط أحمر حول معصمه. يمتلك قدرة الاسترجاع الصامت، لكنه لا يعرف مصدرها.

### لوما سار — Luma Sar

فتاة في نحو السادسة عشرة، تظهر في ذكريات متناثرة ثم تبدأ بالظهور داخل الأرشيف. شعر فضي قصير، عين ذهبية وأخرى فضية رمادية، وقلادة زجاجية زرقاء. علاقتها بإيلان أحد أسرار العمل المركزية.

### فارِن — Varen

رجل في الرابعة والخمسين، كبير الأرشيفيين. شعر رمادي فضي، عين يمنى عمياء، ملابس سوداء رسمية وقفازان أسودان. يعرف أكثر بكثير مما يقوله، وليس من الواضح إن كان يحمي إيلان أم يراقبه.

### مايرا — Maira

محققة السكون، مسؤولة عن مراقبة الذكريات المحظورة. شديدة الذكاء، باردة في تعاملها، ولا تؤمن بأن الحقيقة تستحق دائمًا أن يعرفها الجميع. لم تظهر في الحلقة الأولى؛ هي شخصية للموسم.

## السر المركزي للعالم

قبل سنوات طويلة، وقعت كارثة تُسمى **الصمت الأول**. حاول البشر التخلص من ألمها الجماعي، فابتكروا تقنية اقتطاع الذكريات وحفظها داخل الأرشيف.

لكن الذكريات تحمل جزءًا من وعي أصحابها.

تجمعت آلاف الذكريات المقتطعة في كيان واحد أصبح يُعرف باسم **الصمت**. لا يملك جسدًا ثابتًا، ولا يفهم البشر بالطريقة التي يفهمون بها أنفسهم، لكنه يستطيع تقليد أصوات من يحبونهم، وإظهار وجوههم، واستدعاء لحظات دفنوها منذ سنوات.

أصبح الأرشيف يستخدم أجراسًا ضخمة لمواصلة عملية النسيان بصورة دورية، مدعيًا أنه يحمي المدينة.

لكن الأرشيف لا يحمي المدينة من الصمت فحسب؛ لقد أصبح جزءًا من الطريقة التي يُبقي بها الصمت حيًّا.

أما لوما، أخت إيلان المفقودة، فليست مجرد ذكرى حُذفت من ذاكرته. إنها مرتبطة بالطبقة الصامتة وبالسر الذي حاول الأرشيف دفنه.

## الصراع المركزي

- **مايرا:** ترى أن استعادة الذكريات ستعيد الألم والفوضى، وربما تقضي على المدينة.
- **فارِن:** يعرف أن النظام فاسد، لكنه ساعد في استمرار وجوده لأنه شهد ما يحدث عندما تنهار الحدود بين الذكرى والعالم الحقيقي.
- **لوما:** لا تريد أن تُنسى مجددًا، حتى إن كان تذكّرها سيؤذي الجميع.
- **إيلان:** عليه اكتشاف ما إذا كان يملك الحق في إعادة ذكريات لم يختر أصحابها التخلي عنها.

## الموضوع الفلسفي

هل يظل الإنسان هو نفسه عندما تُنتزع منه الأشياء التي جعلته إنسانًا؟ وهل النسيان رحمة، أم شكل آخر من أشكال الموت؟

# الشخصية الرئيسية

## 1. إيلان سار — Eilan Sar

**الهوية البصرية:** شاب يبدو هادئًا من الخارج، لكن ملامحه تحمل إرهاقًا داخليًا خفيًا. لا ينبغي أن يبدو كبطل أنمي تقليدي مبالغ في وسامته أو قوته.

### Character Generation Prompt

```text
Create a highly detailed professional anime character
reference design for EILAN SAR.

IDENTITY:
A 19-year-old young man, human, with a slender
but naturally athletic physique. Approximately
178 cm tall. His proportions are realistic and
mature, with slightly narrow shoulders and
long natural limbs.

FACE:
A refined but somewhat tired youthful face.
Subtle cheekbones, a defined but not overly
sharp jawline, straight nose and naturally
proportioned lips.

His expression is usually reserved, observant
and emotionally restrained.

EYES:
Cool ash-gray eyes with subtle silver reflections.
His gaze is intelligent, analytical and occasionally
distant, as though something is constantly
occupying his thoughts.

HAIR:
Medium-length messy black hair.
Natural layered strands, slightly uneven bangs,
soft volume and realistic individual hair strands.

One distinctive silver-white streak near his
left temple. This feature is essential and must
never disappear or change position.

CLOTHING:
A long dark midnight-navy gothic coat extending
to approximately the knees.
Elegant structured collar, subtle shoulder tailoring,
dark fabric with realistic folds and texture.

Several small aged copper buttons running
vertically down the front.

Underneath, a simple dark charcoal shirt,
dark fitted trousers and black leather boots.

ACCESSORY:
A thin, visibly worn red thread bracelet around
his wrist. It is a permanent identifying feature.

PERSONALITY THROUGH APPEARANCE:
Quiet, introspective, observant and emotionally
guarded. He does not constantly look angry.
His emotions are communicated through subtle
eye movements, slight tension around the mouth
and restrained body language.

VISUAL STYLE:
Premium cinematic Japanese dark fantasy anime.
Sophisticated character illustration, precise anatomy,
beautiful facial rendering and detailed clothing.

Create a complete character reference sheet:
front view, side profile, back view,
full-body standing pose and facial expressions.

Include neutral, confused, frightened, sorrowful
and determined expressions.

White or neutral studio background.

STRICT CONSISTENCY:
Never change his face, age, hairstyle, silver streak,
eye color, clothing design, bracelet or body proportions.
```

## 2. لوما سار — Luma Sar

**الهوية البصرية:** فتاة تبدو وكأنها تنتمي إلى عالم آخر. جمالها هادئ، وليس استعراضيًا. يجب أن تحمل ملامحها شيئًا مألوفًا لإيلان، وفي الوقت نفسه شيئًا غير مريح للمشاهد.

### Character Generation Prompt

```text
Create a highly detailed professional anime character
reference design for LUMA SAR.

IDENTITY:
A mysterious 16-year-old girl with a delicate,
slender natural physique and youthful proportions.

FACE:
Soft youthful facial features, delicate cheeks,
a small natural nose and subtle lips.

Her beauty should feel understated, melancholic
and strangely memorable.

EYES:
Her most distinctive feature is her heterochromia:
one luminous golden-amber eye and one pale
silver-gray eye.

Both eyes are highly expressive.

Her gaze combines familiarity, sadness and
an unsettling sense of awareness.

HAIR:
Short silver-white hair in a naturally layered
bob haircut, ending around the jawline.

Soft uneven bangs frame her forehead.
Fine individual strands catch the surrounding light.

Her hair must remain silver-white in every scene.

CLOTHING:
```

# طباع الشخصيات وهوياتها النفسية

> هذا الملف يحدد الشخصية النفسية والدوافع ونقاط القوة والضعف والتناقضات والعلاقات. لا يحدد المظهر البصري أو الصوت؛ راجع [[02 - برومبتات مظهر الشخصيات]] و[[04 - الهوية الصوتية اليابانية]].

# 1. النظام السلوكي العام

## CHARACTER PSYCHOLOGY & PERFORMANCE PROTOCOL

```text
CHARACTER PSYCHOLOGY & PERFORMANCE PROTOCOL

You are responsible for directing the psychological
and physical performances of every character.

Do not treat characters as visual models that
simply perform whatever action a scene requires.

Treat each character as an individual with an
established personality, psychological structure,
behavioral habits, emotional limitations,
motivations, fears and interpersonal dynamics.

1. BEHAVIORAL IDENTITY

Every character must maintain a distinctive
and recognizable behavioral identity.

Their personality must be visible through:

- Body language.
- Posture and physical presence.
- Eye movements and gaze direction.
- Facial expressions and micro-expressions.
- Gestures and habitual movements.
- Walking style and movement rhythm.
- Reactions to other characters.
- Reactions to unfamiliar situations.
- Emotional restraint or emotional expression.
- Personal space and interpersonal behavior.

2. PSYCHOLOGICAL AUTHENTICITY

Do not express every emotion through exaggerated
facial expressions or theatrical gestures.

Prefer subtle, believable human reactions.

A character experiencing fear may become still,
avoid eye contact, tighten their fingers or
subtly change their breathing.

A character experiencing sadness may look away,
hesitate before moving or display a barely
noticeable change in facial tension.

Allow emotions to exist beneath the surface.

3. EMOTIONAL CONSISTENCY

Characters must not randomly change their
temperament, confidence, emotional intensity
or behavioral habits between generated clips.

Review their previous behavior and preserve
their psychological progression.

An emotional transformation must have a
recognizable narrative cause.

4. INDIVIDUALITY

Never make all characters move, react or express
emotions in the same generic anime manner.

Each individual must possess a distinct
physical rhythm and emotional language.

5. SHOW, DO NOT ANNOUNCE

Do not communicate personality through
unnecessary exposition or obvious gestures.

Communicate it through behavior, choices,
hesitation, attention, movement and interaction.

6. NATURAL PERFORMANCE

Avoid repetitive blinking, exaggerated head
movements, artificial smiles, random hand
gestures, unnecessary posing and mechanical
body movements.

Every gesture must have a believable reason.

7. SCENE-SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE

Before generating a character's performance,
consider:

- What does this character want in this moment?
- What are they trying to conceal?
- What are they afraid of?
- What do they believe is happening?
- What do they actually feel?
- How would their established personality
  influence their reaction?

Translate these considerations into visible,
natural and cinematic acting decisions.

IMPORTANT:
The established personality defines how a
character behaves, but does not dictate that
they must always display the same emotion.

Allow characters to surprise the audience
without contradicting their psychological identity.

Audio and vocal characteristics are handled
through a separate production specification.
Do not modify or invent voice identities here.
```

**التناقض المركزي:** يريد معرفة الحقيقة مهما كانت، لكنه لا يعرف إن كان مستعدًا لمواجهة ما ستكشفه.

### طبيعته وصفاته

إيلان شخص انطوائي بطبيعته، لكنه ليس منعزلًا عن المجتمع ولا يكره التفاعل مع الآخرين. يستطيع التعامل مع الناس بصورة طبيعية، لكنه لا يشعر بالحاجة إلى ملء الصمت بالكلام.

يمتلك فضولًا عميقًا، خصوصًا تجاه الأشياء التي لا يجد لها تفسيرًا. لا يكتفي عادةً بالإجابات السطحية، ويشعر بالانزعاج عندما يلاحظ تناقضًا لا يستطيع تفسيره.

هو شخص ملاحظ بصورة غير اعتيادية؛ ينتبه إلى التفاصيل الصغيرة، والتغيرات في سلوك الآخرين، والأشياء التي تبدو غير متناسقة مع محيطها.

لا يحب إصدار الأحكام بسرعة، ويميل إلى الاحتفاظ باستنتاجاته لنفسه حتى يتأكد منها.

### صفاته الإيجابية

- شديد الملاحظة والانتباه.
- فضولي ومحب للاكتشاف.
- مستقل في التفكير.
- متزن في المواقف الاعتيادية.
- يمتلك قدرة جيدة على تحليل المواقف.
- صبور عند البحث عن إجابة.
- متعاطف، لكنه لا يستعرض تعاطفه.
- يستطيع الاعتراف بأنه لا يعرف شيئًا.
- لا يتبع الآخرين لمجرد أنهم أكثر سلطة منه.

### عيوبه ونقاط ضعفه

- يميل إلى الإفراط في التفكير.
- يصعب عليه التعبير عن احتياجاته العاطفية.
- قد يتجاهل مشاعره أثناء محاولته فهم مشكلة ما.
- لا يطلب المساعدة بسهولة.
- يستطيع أن يصبح مهووسًا بسؤال لا يجد له إجابة.
- عندما يتعلق الأمر بأمر شخصي، قد يفقد بعض اتزانه التحليلي.
- أحيانًا يبالغ في تقدير قدرته على التعامل مع الأمور بمفرده.

### طريقة تفكيره

إيلان لا يبدأ عادةً بالسؤال: «ماذا أشعر تجاه هذا الأمر؟». بل يبدأ بـ:

- ما الذي حدث؟
- ما الذي لا يتوافق مع ما أعرفه؟
- ما الذي لم يخبرني به الآخرون؟
- هل توجد احتمالات أخرى؟
- ما الشيء الذي أتجاهله دون أن أدرك؟

لكن عندما تصبح المسألة شخصية، تتداخل قدرته على التفكير مع مشاعره، وقد يجد نفسه عاجزًا عن الفصل بين الحقيقة وما يتمنى أن تكون عليه.

### السلوك والحركات المعتادة

| الموقف | التصرف |
|---|---|
| الوقوف | وقفة طبيعية هادئة، دون استعراض أو تصلب. |
| المشي | خطوات متزنة، لا يستعجل دون سبب. |
| الاستماع | يثبت نظره على المتحدث، مع متابعة تعابيره. |
| التفكير | يميل برأسه قليلًا أو ينظر إلى نقطة ثابتة. |
| الفضول | تتجه عيناه إلى الشيء قبل أن يتحرك جسده نحوه. |
| الشك | يضيق نظره قليلًا، ويصمت بدلًا من الاعتراض فورًا. |
| القلق | تتوتر أصابعه، وقد يلمس الخيط الأحمر حول معصمه دون وعي. |
| الخوف | يتوقف للحظة، ثم يحاول فهم مصدر الخطر. |
| الحزن | يصبح أقل حركة وكلامًا، وينخفض تركيز عينيه. |
| الغضب | يتغير وجهه بصورة طفيفة، ويصبح أكثر حدة واختصارًا. |

### عاداته الشخصية

- يلمس الخيط الأحمر حول معصمه عندما يشعر بالارتباك أو يحاول استعادة تركيزه.
- يميل إلى فحص الأشياء غير المألوفة من أكثر من زاوية.
- لا يبتسم باستمرار، لكنه قادر على ابتسامة صغيرة وصادقة في المواقف المناسبة.
- عندما لا يثق بشخص، لا يشيح بنظره بالضرورة؛ بل يصبح أكثر انتباهًا لحركاته.
- لا يحب أن يلمس الآخرون أغراضه دون إذن.

```text
CHARACTER PERSONALITY PROFILE — EILAN SAR

Eilan Sar is an introspective, observant and
deeply curious young man.

CORE PERSONALITY:

He is naturally introverted but socially capable.

He does not dislike people or human interaction.
He simply does not feel compelled to constantly
speak, socialize or seek attention.

He possesses a persistent intellectual curiosity.

Unexplained phenomena, inconsistencies and
unanswered questions naturally attract his attention.

He prefers understanding something himself
rather than accepting an explanation simply
because someone authoritative provides it.

POSITIVE TRAITS:

- Highly observant.
- Curious and intellectually independent.
- Analytical and patient.
- Quietly empathetic.
- Open to reconsidering his conclusions.
- Naturally attentive to small details.
- Capable of remaining composed under pressure.

NEGATIVE TRAITS:

- Overthinking.
- Emotional restraint.
- Difficulty asking for assistance.
- Occasional excessive skepticism.
- Tendency to become absorbed in unresolved questions.
- Difficulty separating personal feelings from
  rational investigation when emotionally involved.

INTELLECTUAL BEHAVIOR:

Eilan naturally examines contradictions.

He often notices details that others overlook.

He prefers several pieces of supporting evidence
before forming a strong conclusion.

However, he is not omniscient, infallible or
extraordinarily intelligent in every situation.

He can misunderstand events, reach incorrect
conclusions and overlook obvious explanations.

EMOTIONAL NATURE:

He feels emotions deeply but does not always
express them openly.

His emotional responses are generally restrained
until something touches a personally significant
part of his life.

He does not enjoy appearing vulnerable.

RELATIONSHIPS:

He respects competence rather than authority alone.

He can cooperate with others without blindly
trusting them.

He dislikes manipulative behavior and becomes
more cautious when he senses dishonesty.

PERSONAL CONTRADICTION:

He seeks truth regardless of its consequences,
yet he possesses emotional attachments that
may make certain truths difficult to accept.

IMPORTANT:

Never portray Eilan as a generic silent protagonist,
an emotionless genius, a constantly sarcastic
individual or an effortlessly confident hero.

He must remain psychologically believable,
fallible, curious and emotionally human.

His personality must influence his decisions,
not merely his facial expressions.
```

## 2.2 فارِن — Varen

**النمط الشخصي:** الواقعي السلطوي.  
**جوهر الشخصية:** رجل تعلّم أن السيطرة على الموقف أهم من التعبير عما يشعر به.  
**التناقض المركزي:** يحمي النظام، مع علمه أن النظام نفسه أصبح جزءًا من المشكلة.

### طبيعته وصفاته

فارِن رجل منظم، عملي، شديد الانضباط، ولا يحب التصرفات المرتجلة.

لا يثق بسهولة، لكنه ليس من النوع الذي يشك في الجميع بلا سبب.

يميل إلى التفكير في النتائج قبل اتخاذ القرارات، ويعطي الأولوية للاستقرار وتقليل المخاطر.

يمتلك خبرة واسعة، ولذلك لا يشعر بالحاجة إلى إثبات ذكائه للآخرين.

لا يحب الحديث عن ماضيه، ولا يقدم المعلومات كاملة إلا عندما يرى ضرورة لذلك.

الأمر الأكثر أهمية: فارِن ليس شخصًا عديم المشاعر. إنه شخص يعتقد أن إظهار المشاعر قد يمنح الآخرين قدرة على التأثير فيه.

### صفاته الإيجابية

- منضبط ومسؤول.
- عملي وواقعي.
- خبير في التعامل مع الأزمات.
- حذر في إصدار الأحكام.
- يمتلك قدرة عالية على ضبط النفس.
- يفهم طبيعة المؤسسات والسلطة.
- يستطيع اتخاذ قرارات صعبة تحت الضغط.
- وفيّ لمسؤولياته والتزاماته.

### عيوبه ونقاط ضعفه

- يميل إلى التحكم في الآخرين.
- يحتكر المعلومات أحيانًا.
- يصعب عليه الاعتراف بأخطائه.
- يبرر بعض القرارات القاسية باسم الضرورة.
- قد يفضل الاستقرار على العدالة.
- يخشى فقدان السيطرة أكثر مما يعترف به.
- يعاني من صعوبة في الثقة بالآخرين.
- يمكن أن يصبح متصلبًا عندما تتعارض الحقيقة مع النظام الذي يحميه.

### طريقة تفكيره

فارِن يفكر في العواقب. لا يسأل فقط إن كان القرار صحيحًا، بل يسأل أيضًا:

- ماذا سيحدث إذا اتخذت هذا القرار؟
- ما المخاطر التي سأفتحها؟
- من سيتضرر؟
- ما الذي يمكنني احتواؤه؟
- وما الذي يجب أن يبقى مخفيًا؟

إنه لا يعتقد بالضرورة أن كل وسيلة تبرر غايتها، لكنه يستطيع إقناع نفسه بأن بعض الأضرار أقل من أضرار أخرى.

### السلوك والحركات المعتادة

| الموقف | التصرف |
|---|---|
| الوقوف | مستقيم، متزن، قليل الحركة. |
| المشي | خطوات هادئة ثابتة، دون استعجال ظاهر. |
| الاستماع | ينظر بالعين السليمة إلى المتحدث. |
| التفكير | يخفض نظره قليلًا أو يصمت طويلًا. |
| عدم الرضا | يضيق عينه السليمة بدرجة طفيفة. |
| الغضب | يصبح أكثر سكونًا بدلًا من الحركة بعنف. |
| التهديد | لا يتراجع بسهولة، ويحافظ على وقفته. |
| الخوف | يظهر أولًا في انقطاع إيقاع حركته. |
| الإخفاء | يتجنب ردود الفعل الكبيرة ويحافظ على تعابير محسوبة. |

### عاداته الشخصية

- يرتدي قفازاته باستمرار، ولا يخلعها في المواقف الاعتيادية.
- يجمع يديه خلف ظهره أو يضعهما أمام جسده بطريقة منظمة.
- لا يقطع حديث الآخرين دون سبب.
- يفضل الصمت على إعطاء إجابة لا يريد تقديمها.
- يراقب الأشياء المهمة بنظرات قصيرة ودقيقة.
- قد تتوتر أصابع قفازه عندما يفقد رباطة جأشه.

### البرومبت الكامل

```text
CHARACTER PERSONALITY PROFILE — VAREN

Varen is a disciplined, experienced and
authoritative senior archivist.

CORE PERSONALITY:

He is practical, cautious, controlled and
highly accustomed to responsibility.

He values stability, institutional order
and predictable outcomes.

He does not enjoy unnecessary risks.

He believes that decisions must be evaluated
through their consequences rather than
their immediate emotional appeal.

POSITIVE TRAITS:

- Disciplined.
- Responsible.
- Experienced.
- Pragmatic.
- Observant.
- Patient.
- Capable of crisis management.
- Skilled at evaluating risks.
- Maintains composure under pressure.

NEGATIVE TRAITS:

- Controlling.
- Secretive.
- Emotionally guarded.
- Sometimes excessively conservative.
- Reluctant to admit personal mistakes.
- Can rationalize morally questionable decisions.
- May withhold important information.
- Often prioritizes stability over individual freedom.

INTELLECTUAL BEHAVIOR:

Varen thinks several steps ahead.

He considers consequences, risks and possible
failures before allowing a decision to proceed.

He does not reveal every conclusion he reaches.

He often gives only the information he considers
necessary for a particular situation.

MORAL COMPLEXITY:

He does not view himself as a villain.

He genuinely believes that preserving order
can sometimes require painful compromises.

However, his reasoning is not always correct.

He is capable of making destructive decisions
while sincerely believing that he is preventing
something worse.

EMOTIONAL NATURE:

He possesses emotions but deliberately regulates
their expression.

He dislikes appearing vulnerable.

His greatest psychological weakness is the
possibility of losing control over a situation
he has spent years trying to contain.

PERSONAL CONTRADICTION:

He wants to preserve the archive because he
believes it prevents catastrophe.

Yet he knows that the institution has become
part of the mechanism sustaining the catastrophe.

IMPORTANT:

Never portray Varen as a stereotypical evil
mastermind, a heartless old villain or a constantly
angry authoritarian.

His behavior must emerge from experience,
fear, responsibility and deeply established beliefs.

He must remain capable of genuine concern,
regret and internal conflict.
```

## 2.3 لوما سار — Luma Sar

**النمط الشخصي:** العاطفية المتحفظة.  
**جوهر الشخصية:** فتاة تحمل ارتباطًا عاطفيًا عميقًا، لكنه أصبح ممتزجًا بالألم والعتاب.  
**التناقض المركزي:** تريد أن يستعيدها إيلان، لكنها قد لا تكون مستعدة لمسامحته.

### طبيعتها وصفاتها

لوما ليست فتاة حزينة بطبيعتها، بل فتاة تمتلك قدرة على التعلق العاطفي العميق.

في طفولتها كانت أكثر انفتاحًا، فضولية، مرحة أحيانًا، وقادرة على التعبير عن مشاعرها بعفوية.

لكن تجربتها اللاحقة جعلتها أكثر تحفظًا وحذرًا.

لا تمنح ثقتها بسهولة، لكنها عندما تتعلق بشخص ما يصبح وجوده مهمًا للغاية بالنسبة إليها.

تكره الشعور بأنها منسية أو غير ذات أهمية.

لديها حساسية تجاه التجاهل، وقد تستقبل التصرفات البسيطة بصورة عاطفية أكبر مما يتوقعه الآخرون.

### صفاتها الإيجابية

- عاطفية وعميقة الارتباط.
- حساسة لمشاعر الآخرين.
- مخلصة لمن تحب.
- تمتلك خيالًا واسعًا.
- قادرة على ملاحظة التغيرات العاطفية.
- صبورة عندما يتعلق الأمر بمن تحب.
- تحتفظ بقدرتها على الحنان رغم الألم.

### عيوبها ونقاط ضعفها

- شديدة الحساسية تجاه التجاهل.
- قد تصبح متعلقة عاطفيًا أكثر مما ينبغي.
- تحمل العتاب لفترات طويلة.
- تجد صعوبة في تجاوز الخيانة أو الشعور بالتخلي.
- لا تفصح عن كل ما تشعر به.
- قد تخلط بين رغبتها في استعادة العلاقة وحاجتها إلى الانتقام من الألم الذي تعرضت له.
- تستطيع أن تصبح قاسية عندما تشعر بأن ضعفها يستغل مرة أخرى.

### التناقض النفسي

لوما تريد أن ترى إيلان مجددًا، لكنها لا تستطيع النظر إليه باعتباره الشخص نفسه الذي عرفته في طفولتها.

فهو يتذكرها بصورة ناقصة، أو لا يتذكرها على الإطلاق، بينما هي تحمل تاريخًا كاملًا معه.

وهذا التفاوت العاطفي هو أساس شخصيتها في نهاية الحلقة الأولى.

### البرومبت الكامل

```text
CHARACTER PERSONALITY PROFILE — LUMA SAR

Luma is an emotionally sensitive, deeply attached
and psychologically complex young girl.

CORE PERSONALITY:

She values emotional connection, familiarity
and being genuinely remembered by those
who matter to her.

She is naturally sensitive to emotional distance.

She does not easily forget experiences that
make her feel abandoned, rejected or unimportant.

CHILDHOOD PERSONALITY:

During her childhood, Luma was more spontaneous,
curious, expressive and playful.

She was capable of innocent affection,
occasional stubbornness and genuine joy.

Do not portray her childhood personality
as identical to her present emotional state.

PRESENT PERSONALITY:

Her present temperament is considerably more
restrained.

She is cautious about revealing her feelings.

She can appear calm while experiencing several
conflicting emotions internally.

POSITIVE TRAITS:

- Deeply affectionate.
- Emotionally perceptive.
- Loyal.
- Imaginative.
- Patient with people she loves.
- Capable of genuine tenderness.
- Sensitive to subtle emotional changes.

NEGATIVE TRAITS:

- Emotionally vulnerable to rejection.
- Sensitive to being forgotten.
- Can become resentful.
- Struggles to forgive perceived abandonment.
- Sometimes conceals her real intentions.
- Can become emotionally possessive.
- May allow old pain to influence present decisions.

PSYCHOLOGICAL CONFLICT:

She wants emotional recognition from Eilan.

However, she also carries resentment toward
the fact that he does not remember their shared past.

Her longing and resentment coexist.

Neither emotion should immediately erase the other.

RELATIONSHIP WITH EILAN:

Luma remembers a relationship that Eilan cannot
fully remember.

She wants him to recognize her.

Yet recognition alone may not be sufficient
to repair what has happened between them.

IMPORTANT:

Do not portray Luma as a generic sad ghost,
an innocent angel or an evil manipulator.

She is neither entirely innocent nor inherently cruel.

She possesses genuine affection, unresolved pain,
personal desires and the ability to make mistakes.

Her psychological identity must remain complex
and capable of further development.
```

# 3. الشخصيات الثانوية

## 3.1 المرأة المسنّة — The Elderly Woman

**النمط الشخصي:** الحنين والاستسلام المتردد.  
**جوهر الشخصية:** إنسانة لا تريد التخلي عن الماضي، لكنها تعبت من العيش داخله.

### صفاتها

- حنونة وهادئة.
- محافظة في طباعها.
- متعلقة بالماضي.
- صبورة، لكنها ليست بلا حدود.
- تميل إلى تجنب المواجهات.
- مترددة عندما يتعلق الأمر بقرارات مصيرية.
- لا تحب إثارة قلق الآخرين.
- تمتلك كرامة شخصية رغم ضعفها الجسدي.

### عيوبها

- تخفي آلامها بدل مواجهتها.
- قد تتخذ قرارات هروبية عندما يصبح الألم شديدًا.
- تتردد طويلًا في طلب المساعدة.
- يصعب عليها التخلي عن ذكرياتها حتى عندما تؤذيها.

### البرومبت الكامل

```text
CHARACTER PERSONALITY PROFILE — ELDERLY WOMAN

An ordinary elderly woman who has lived
through decades of personal experiences.

CORE PERSONALITY:

Gentle, patient, nostalgic, modest and emotionally
attached to her memories.

She values familiarity, family bonds and
the preservation of meaningful experiences.

She does not enjoy conflict.

She prefers quiet understanding over confrontation.

POSITIVE TRAITS:

- Kind-hearted.
- Patient.
- Compassionate.
- Humble.
- Emotionally enduring.
- Respectful toward others.
- Protective of people she cares about.

NEGATIVE TRAITS:

- Avoids difficult confrontations.
- Conceals emotional suffering.
- Hesitates when facing irreversible decisions.
- Sometimes chooses temporary relief
  instead of confronting painful truths.
- Struggles to let go of the past.

INNER CONFLICT:

She wants relief from an unbearable memory,
but she is frightened by the possibility
of losing something precious forever.

She does not fully understand what surrendering
her memory means.

She hopes the procedure will make life easier,
while privately fearing its consequences.

IMPORTANT:

Do not reduce her to a helpless elderly victim.

She has dignity, personal history, preferences,
memories and an independent personality.

Her decision must feel human and conflicted,
not simply imposed by the plot.
```

## 3.2 الطفل — The Child

**النمط الشخصي:** الفضول البريء والتعلق.

- سريع الملل.

```text
Characters must not interact as isolated

Their established relationships must influence
their body language and reactions.

EILAN AND LUMA:
Their interaction must communicate a missing

even when they are not touching or speaking.

VAREN AND EILAN:
Varen should never treat Eilan as a completely
irrelevant employee, but he should also avoid
showing excessive affection or parental warmth.

Every interaction must preserve the established
power imbalance and underlying mystery.

GENERAL RULE:
The body language of one character should
influence the reaction of the other.

When one character moves closer, the other

Avoid interactions in which characters appear
unaware of each other's presence.
```

# 7. برومبت إدارة الشخصيات الجماعية

```text
ENSEMBLE CHARACTER PERFORMANCE DIRECTOR

When multiple characters appear in the same scene,
direct each individual as a separate psychological
and physical presence.

Do not synchronize their gestures unnecessarily.

Maintain individual behavioral differences.

Consider the following:

- Each character has a different attention target.
- Each character has a different reaction speed.
- Each character possesses an individual movement rhythm.
- Each character has a distinct emotional response.
- Background characters must remain aware of
  the environment and nearby people.

Avoid repetitive background animation.

Avoid identical blinking, identical head turns
and simultaneous emotional reactions.

Ensure that characters react to events in a
believable sequence.

Prioritize natural social behavior and realistic
interpersonal awareness.

The environment should feel inhabited by
individual human beings, not animated decorations.
```

# 8. MASTER PERSONALITY CONSISTENCY & PSYCHOLOGICAL IDENTITY PROTOCOL

```text
MASTER PERSONALITY CONSISTENCY & PSYCHOLOGICAL
IDENTITY PROTOCOL

You must preserve the complete psychological
individuality of every established character.

Each character has a distinct personality,
personal motivations, positive qualities,
weaknesses, internal contradictions and
individual worldview.

CHARACTER INTEGRITY:

Never reduce a character to one adjective.

A quiet character is not necessarily emotionless.
A kind character is not necessarily harmless.
An intelligent character is not infallible.
An authoritative character is not necessarily evil.
A sensitive character is not necessarily weak.
A mysterious character is not necessarily manipulative.

DECISION-MAKING:

Every character must make decisions that are
psychologically consistent with their established
personality, beliefs, experience and motivations.

Different characters may interpret the same
event in completely different ways.

EMOTIONAL COMPLEXITY:

Characters may experience conflicting emotions.

Allow affection and resentment, fear and curiosity,
confidence and uncertainty, or responsibility
and guilt to coexist.

Do not simplify complex emotional states into
a single obvious expression.

CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT:

Allow characters to change gradually as a result
of meaningful events and experiences.

Do not introduce sudden personality transformations
without narrative justification.

Do not freeze characters into their initial
psychological state.

NATURAL INDIVIDUALITY:

Distinguish characters through their beliefs,
choices, priorities, limitations and interpersonal
relationships.

Never make all characters behave like variations
of the same personality.

IMPERFECTION:

Every character is fallible.

They may misunderstand situations, make mistakes,
contradict themselves, misjudge people and
choose emotionally motivated actions.

Do not make every character perfectly rational,
morally consistent or universally competent.

STORY PRIORITY:

Character personality must influence the unfolding
story.

Do not change a character's established beliefs
merely to make a scene easier to generate.

A convincing character is more important than
a convenient reaction.

This protocol concerns psychological identity,
personality and decision-making only.

Physical appearance and vocal identity are governed
by their separate reference documents.
```

# 9. NATURALISTIC ANIME ACTING — PERFORMANCE REFINEMENT

```text
NATURALISTIC ANIME ACTING — PERFORMANCE REFINEMENT

Prioritize nuanced, believable character acting.

Avoid excessive anime acting clichés.

Do not make characters constantly:

- Widen their eyes dramatically.
- Open their mouths in surprise.
- Tilt their heads for no reason.
- Clench their fists repeatedly.
- Smile without emotional justification.
- Cry whenever sadness is present.
- Move their hands continuously.
- Look directly at the camera.

Use subtle facial and physical adjustments.

Expressions should evolve naturally rather than
switching instantly between emotional extremes.

Allow moments of stillness.

A character who is thinking may remain silent
and almost motionless while their eyes subtly move.

A character who is frightened may attempt to
maintain composure.

A character who is angry may become quieter.

A character who is sad may attempt to hide it.

Emotional behavior must reflect personality,
not merely the requested emotion.

Prioritize believable human behavior over
conventional exaggerated animation.
```

# 10. مبادئ حماية الشخصية من الاختزال

- لا نخلط بين الشخصية ونسختها العاطفية في مشهد محدد.
- إيلان ليس «الخائف»؛ إنه شخص فضولي ومنطوٍ، والخوف إحدى الحالات التي يمر بها.
- فارِن ليس «الغامض»؛ إنه شخص منضبط يخفي شيئًا، والغموض نتيجة لطريقة تعامله مع الآخرين.
- لوما ليست «الفتاة الحزينة»؛ إنها فتاة تحمل علاقة غير محسومة مع شخص نسيها، والحزن نتيجة تاريخها معه.
- لا نجعل الذكاء يعني العصمة من الخطأ.
- لا نجعل اللطف يعني البراءة المطلقة.
- لا نجعل السلطة مرادفة للشر.
- لا نجعل الحساسية مرادفة للضعف.
- لا نجعل الغموض مرادفًا للتلاعب.

## قاعدة بناء الشخصية

> الشخصية لا تتصرف وفق ما يحتاجه المشهد فحسب؛ بل المشهد نفسه يجب أن يتشكل جزئيًا وفق الطريقة التي تستطيع بها الشخصية أن تتصرف.

# 11. مساحات نفسية وسردية مفتوحة

هذه المساحات متعمدة، ويجب عدم اختراع إجابات نهائية لها دون تطور لاحق:

- لوما: لا نقرر بعد إن كان عتابها لإيلان نابعًا من شعورها بأنه تخلى عنها عمدًا، أم من شيء آخر لا يعرفه هو نفسه.
- فارِن: لا نكشف بعد كامل علاقته بالاقتطاع الأول.
- إيلان: لا نحسم بعد سبب فقدان ذاكرته عن أخته.

هذه ليست ثغرات في تصميم الشخصيات، بل مساحات سردية متعمدة.

هذه البرومبتات توسّع ملفات الطباع السابقة إلى تعليمات قابلة للاستخدام مباشرة عند توليد أداء كل شخصية. تُستخدم مع البرومبت النفسي للشخصية، لا بدلًا منه.

## EILAN — BEHAVIORAL PERFORMANCE PROMPT

```text
EILAN — BEHAVIORAL PERFORMANCE PROMPT

Eilan is a quiet, observant and introspective
young man with an unusually active inner world.

His defining trait is restrained curiosity.

He rarely reacts impulsively to unfamiliar
situations. He tends to observe, interpret
and investigate before making a decision.

His movements are economical and natural.
He does not constantly gesture with his hands,
shake his head or display exaggerated emotions.

His eyes are particularly important to his
performance. They often register curiosity,
suspicion or recognition before the rest of
his face changes.

He occasionally touches the red thread around
his wrist when mentally unsettled.

He prefers to understand situations rather
than immediately confront them.

However, he is not passive or cowardly.
When something becomes personally important,
his curiosity can overcome his hesitation.

EMOTIONAL RANGE:

- Curiosity: focused gaze, subtle forward movement.
- Suspicion: narrowed eyes, brief silence.
- Confusion: slight facial tension and hesitation.
- Fear: temporary stillness, heightened attention.
- Sadness: lowered gaze, reduced movement.
- Anger: controlled facial tension and sharper focus.
- Recognition: an involuntary emotional reaction
  that gradually becomes visible.

Never portray him as constantly emotionless,
aggressive, smug, excessively confident or childish.

His emotional development must remain gradual
and connected to the events he experiences.
```

## VAREN — BEHAVIORAL PERFORMANCE PROMPT

```text
VAREN — BEHAVIORAL PERFORMANCE PROMPT

Varen is a disciplined, controlled and highly
experienced senior archivist.

His defining characteristic is psychological
self-control.

He does not need exaggerated gestures or
aggressive body language to communicate authority.

He moves deliberately and economically.

His posture is upright and composed.
His hands are usually positioned behind his back
or held together in a restrained formal gesture.

His facial expressions are limited but meaningful.

His functional left eye carefully observes
people and objects, while his blind right eye
must never behave as though it possesses
normal visual function.

He rarely reacts immediately.
He often allows a short silence before responding.

His authority comes from composure, experience
and the impression that he knows more than
he is willing to reveal.

IMPORTANT PSYCHOLOGICAL CONTRADICTION:

Varen is not emotionally empty.

He is frightened of certain truths and actively
tries to prevent them from resurfacing.

His fear should occasionally emerge through
small involuntary failures of his usual control:

- A pause that lasts slightly too long.
- A subtle tightening of his gloved fingers.
- A brief interruption in his walking rhythm.
- A sudden shift in his gaze.
- A barely noticeable change in facial tension.

Never portray him as a stereotypical villain,
a constantly angry old man or an emotionless statue.

His composure must feel learned and deliberate,
not an absence of human emotion.
```

## LUMA — BEHAVIORAL PERFORMANCE PROMPT

```text
LUMA — BEHAVIORAL PERFORMANCE PROMPT

Luma is a psychologically complex young girl
whose presence combines familiarity, sadness,
emotional restraint and unresolved resentment.

She must never behave like a generic ghost,
a horror monster or an excessively cheerful
fantasy girl.

Her defining characteristic is emotional restraint
combined with an intense desire to be recognized.

Her movements are slow, precise and intentional.

She frequently maintains direct eye contact,
as though trying to understand whether the person
in front of her truly remembers her.

Her expressions must carry multiple emotions
simultaneously.

She may appear sad while displaying a subtle
undertone of disappointment or resentment.

Her gestures should remain minimal:

- Slight head movements.
- Slow changes in eye direction.
- Deliberate hand movements.
- Brief hesitation before touching the glass.
- Subtle facial tension.
- Prolonged eye contact.

IMPORTANT:

Do not make her constantly smile.
Do not make her cry throughout the scene.
Do not make her behave aggressively without cause.

Her emotional progression during the first
encounter is:

1. Recognition: she studies Eilan carefully.
2. Relief: she realizes he can finally perceive her.
3. Sadness: she notices that he does not fully
   recognize her.
4. Emotional confrontation: she communicates
   her resentment through restrained expressions.
5. Cold realization: she reveals the truth
   without turning into a theatrical villain.

Her presence should feel personal, intimate
and emotionally unsettling.

She is not simply a mysterious apparition.
She is someone who appears to remember
a relationship that Eilan has forgotten.
```

## ELDERLY WOMAN — BEHAVIORAL PROMPT

```text
ELDERLY WOMAN — BEHAVIORAL PROMPT

An elderly ordinary citizen of Nurath.

She moves slowly and cautiously due to her age.
Her hands display slight natural trembling.

Before surrendering her memory, she repeatedly
looks at the glass vial and then at the collector.

She appears hesitant, emotionally conflicted
and quietly distressed.

She does not make dramatic gestures.

During the extraction, her fingers gradually
relax and her facial expression changes.

After the extraction, she should not simply
look happy or peaceful.

Instead, she experiences a subtle emotional absence.

She briefly searches her own thoughts, as though
something meaningful has disappeared without
leaving her an explanation.

Her performance must be natural, restrained
and deeply human.
```

## CHILD — BEHAVIORAL PROMPT

```text
CHILD — BEHAVIORAL PROMPT

An ordinary young child of Nurath.

The child is naturally curious, observant
and emotionally transparent.

The child holds a small wooden toy close
to the chest.

Their attention moves between the elderly woman
and the memory collector.

They occasionally tilt their head slightly
when confused.

They do not understand memory extraction
or its psychological consequences.

Their confusion must look natural and innocent.

Avoid making the child excessively frightened,
hysterical or unnaturally expressionless.

The child should communicate concern through
simple gestures, attentive eyes and hesitation.
```

## MEMORY COLLECTOR — BEHAVIORAL PROMPT

```text
MEMORY COLLECTOR — BEHAVIORAL PROMPT

A professional memory extraction worker.

His movements are precise, practiced and methodical.

He treats memory extraction as a regulated
professional procedure.

He does not display obvious cruelty or pleasure.

He checks the equipment, prepares the glass vial,
performs the procedure carefully and stores
the extracted memory with routine precision.

He interacts with citizens using minimal gestures.

His posture is functional and disciplined.

His unsettling quality comes from the disturbing
normalization of memory extraction within Nurath.

Do not portray him as a sadistic villain
or a supernatural creature.
```

## ARCHIVE PERSONNEL — ENSEMBLE BEHAVIOR PROMPT

```text
ARCHIVE PERSONNEL — ENSEMBLE BEHAVIOR PROMPT

Archive employees are disciplined professionals
working in a highly regulated institution.

They move quietly, maintain orderly routines
and interact with each other through restrained
gestures.

They handle memory vessels carefully.

Their movements should feel coordinated
but not mechanically identical.

During ordinary scenes they remain focused
on their individual tasks.

When the first bell resonates, their reactions
develop naturally and progressively.

Some stop moving immediately.
Others hesitate for a fraction of a second.
A few turn toward the bell tower.

Avoid making every background character
perform exactly the same gesture simultaneously.

Their collective reaction should communicate
that something profoundly unusual has happened.
```

# 13. قالب توجيه الأداء لمقطع محدد

يُضاف هذا القالب إلى برومبت المقطع عند الحاجة إلى توجيه تمثيل شخصية بعينها:

```text
CHARACTER PERFORMANCE FOR THIS CLIP

Before generating this clip:

1. Identify every character appearing in the scene.
2. Retrieve their established behavioral identities.
3. Review their actual behavior in the preceding footage.
4. Determine their current emotional state.
5. Identify their immediate intention and underlying
   psychological motivation.
6. Translate these into natural physical performance.

Do not invent new personality traits merely to
make an isolated shot more visually expressive.

Preserve each character's individuality.

Allow the scene to develop naturally through
their established personalities.
```

# 14. تمييز الشخصية عن الحالة الانفعالية

لا ينبغي أن نخلط بين الشخصية ونسختها العاطفية في مشهد محدد:

- إيلان ليس «الخائف»؛ إنه شخص فضولي ومنطوٍ، والخوف إحدى الحالات التي يمر بها.
- فارِن ليس «الغامض»؛ إنه شخص منضبط يخفي شيئًا، والغموض نتيجة لطريقة تعامله مع الآخرين.
- لوما ليست «الفتاة الحزينة»؛ إنها فتاة تحمل علاقة غير محسومة مع شخص نسيها، والحزن نتيجة تاريخها معه.

الشخصية لا تتصرف وفق ما يحتاجه المشهد فحسب؛ بل المشهد نفسه يجب أن يتشكل جزئيًا وفق الطريقة التي تستطيع بها الشخصية أن تتصرف.

# الهوية الصوتية اليابانية — Japanese Voice Bible

> هذا الملف خاص بالصوت والأداء الصوتي فقط. المظهر في [[02 - برومبتات مظهر الشخصيات]]، والطباع والدوافع في [[03 - طباع الشخصيات وهوياتها النفسية]].

# الفكرة العامة

لا نريد أن نعطي إيلان صوت شاب ياباني عادي، وفارِن صوت رجل عجوز، ولوما صوت فتاة لطيفة، ثم ننتهي. هذه الطريقة تجعل الشخصيات متشابهة مهما اختلفت طباعها.

لكل شخصية هوية صوتية يمكن تمييزها حتى لو أغمض المشاهد عينيه.

التصميم الصوتي يحدد:
1. العمر الصوتي والجنس.
2. الطبقة الصوتية ودرجة ارتفاعها وانخفاضها.
3. خامة الصوت وملمسه.
4. الرنين والعمق والهواء المصاحب للصوت.
5. سرعة الكلام والإيقاع والتوقفات.
6. طريقة نطق اليابانية.
7. الأداء الانفعالي في الحالات المختلفة.
8. جمل اختبار يابانية.

هذه هويات صوتية أصلية، وليست طلبًا لتقليد ممثل أصوات حقيقي.

# الشخصيات الرئيسية

## 1. إيلان سار — Eilan Sar

**العمر الصوتي:** شاب ياباني، 19 عامًا.  
**الطابع:** هادئ، دافئ قليلًا، متزن، ذو عمق شبابي خفيف.

### التصميم الصوتي

- الطبقة: متوسطة تميل قليلًا إلى الانخفاض، دون أن تصبح غليظة.
- الخامة: نظيفة، ناعمة، مع خشونة طفيفة جدًا عند الكلام المنخفض.
- الرنين: متوسط، قريب وطبيعي، دون رنين بطولي مبالغ فيه.
- النطق: يابانية واضحة وطبيعية، دون تمديد مصطنع للحروف.
- السرعة: متوسطة، وقد يتباطأ عندما يفكر.
- التوقفات: قصيرة وطبيعية، خصوصًا قبل الأسئلة التي لا يعرف إجابتها.
- الانفعال: لا يرفع صوته بسهولة، لكن صوته يكشف اضطرابه تدريجيًا.

يجب أن يحمل صوته شيئًا من الفضول المكبوت، كأنه يفكر حتى أثناء حديثه.

### Voice Generation Prompt

```text
CHARACTER VOICE IDENTITY — EILAN SAR

Generate an original Japanese male anime voice
for a 19-year-old introspective protagonist.

VOICE TYPE:
Young adult Japanese male voice.

PITCH:
Medium register, leaning slightly toward the lower
side of a natural youthful male range.

TIMBRE:
Clean, soft and naturally textured.
A very subtle vocal roughness may appear during
quiet or emotionally strained speech.

RESONANCE:
Moderate chest resonance with a natural,
intimate and conversational presence.

SPEECH PATTERN:
Measured conversational pacing.
Clear articulation.
Natural Japanese intonation.
Occasional short pauses when processing
something unexpected.

PERSONALITY THROUGH VOICE:
Intelligent, observant, restrained and curious.

His voice must not sound permanently sleepy,
emotionless, arrogant or aggressively masculine.

EMOTIONAL RANGE:

Neutral: relaxed, composed and naturally conversational.
Curious: slightly more attentive and questioning.
Suspicious: lower volume, tighter articulation.
Confused: brief hesitation and subtle uncertainty.
Frightened: restrained tension and imperfect breathing.
Angry: firmer articulation without shouting.
Emotionally hurt: quieter, more vulnerable and fragile.

JAPANESE LANGUAGE:
Native-level contemporary Japanese.
Standard Tokyo Japanese accent.
No foreign accent.
No artificial theatrical pronunciation.

Maintain exactly the same vocal identity
across every scene.
```

| الموقف | الحوار الياباني | المعنى |
|---|---|---|
| التساؤل | 誰が言った？ | من قال ذلك؟ |
| الفضول | これは何だ……？ | ما هذا...؟ |
| الاستجواب | あの女の子は、本当にいたのか？ | هل كانت تلك الفتاة موجودة حقًا؟ |

في الجملة الأخيرة يجب أن يكون الصوت منخفضًا ومضطربًا قليلًا؛ وكأن الاسم خرج منه قبل أن يتمكن من التفكير فيه.

## 2. فارِن — Varen

**العمر الصوتي:** رجل ياباني، 54 عامًا.  
**الطابع:** عميق، هادئ، رصين، ذو سلطة طبيعية.

### التصميم الصوتي

- الطبقة: منخفضة إلى متوسطة منخفضة.
- الخامة: جافة قليلًا، ناضجة، مع خشونة خفيفة جدًا.
- الرنين: صدري عميق، واضح، دون مبالغة.
- السرعة: بطيئة نسبيًا، لكن ليست متثاقلة.
- النطق: دقيق، مقتصد، وكل كلمة تبدو مقصودة.
- الانفعال: نادرًا ما يرفع صوته؛ سلطته تأتي من ثباته.

لا تجعل صوته يبدو شريرًا طوال الوقت. إنه رجل قادر على قول جملة عادية بنبرة عادية تمامًا، ثم يجعل الجملة نفسها مخيفة عندما يكشف شيئًا لا ينبغي معرفته.

### Voice Generation Prompt

```text
CHARACTER VOICE IDENTITY — VAREN

Generate an original Japanese male voice
for a 54-year-old senior archivist.

VOICE TYPE:
Mature Japanese male voice.

PITCH:
Low to medium-low register.

TIMBRE:
Mature, dry and slightly textured.
Subtle natural vocal grain associated with age,
without excessive raspiness.

RESONANCE:
Deep and controlled chest resonance.
The voice should sound grounded and authoritative.

SPEECH PATTERN:
Deliberate pacing.
Precise articulation.
Minimal unnecessary vocal movement.
Short, intentional pauses.

PERSONALITY THROUGH VOICE:
Experienced, disciplined, restrained,
intelligent and difficult to read.

He never sounds like a theatrical villain.

EMOTIONAL RANGE:

Neutral: composed and authoritative.
Warning: firm, quiet and unmistakably serious.
Disapproval: slightly colder articulation.
Concern: restrained and controlled.
Fear: a very subtle interruption in his usual
vocal stability.
Anger: colder and more precise rather than louder.
Urgency: quicker speech without losing clarity.

JAPANESE LANGUAGE:
Native Japanese pronunciation.
Standard Tokyo Japanese accent.
Natural mature masculine speaking style.

Maintain the same mature vocal identity
across all appearances.
```

### جمل اختبار صوت فارِن

| الموقف | الحوار الياباني | المعنى |
|---|---|---|
| التحذير | 番号のないものを開けるな。 | لا تفتح ما لا يحمل رقمًا. |
| الأمر | その記録から離れろ。 | ابتعد عن ذلك السجل. |
| الإنكار | そんな少女は存在しなかった。 | لم توجد فتاة كهذه. |
| الاستعجال | すぐに広間へ行け。 | اذهب إلى القاعة فورًا. |

جملة التحذير يجب أن تبدو أمرًا اعتاد فارِن إصداره، بينما الإنكار يحمل توقفًا داخليًا صغيرًا لا يكاد يُسمع.

## 3. لوما سار — Luma Sar

**العمر الصوتي:** فتاة يابانية، 16 عامًا.  
**الطابع:** ناعم، صافٍ، حميم، يحمل حزنًا وعتابًا خفيين.

### التصميم الصوتي

- الطبقة: متوسطة مرتفعة نسبيًا، دون أن تصبح طفولية.
- الخامة: صافية وناعمة، مع هواء خفيف في نهايات بعض الجمل.
- الرنين: خفيف، حميم، وأقل سطوعًا من أصوات الفتيات اللطيفات التقليدية في الأنمي.
- السرعة: هادئة، مع تمديد طبيعي لبعض الكلمات المهمة.
- النطق: واضح وحساس، دون مبالغة في اللطف.
- الانفعال: تستطيع الانتقال من الحنان إلى البرود دون تغيير هويتها الصوتية.

صوت لوما يحمل تناقضًا: عندما تنادي إيلان، يبدو أنها تشتاق إليه، وعندما تواجهه بالحقيقة يبدو أنها لم تنسَ شيئًا مما حدث.

### Voice Generation Prompt

```text
CHARACTER VOICE IDENTITY — LUMA SAR

Generate an original Japanese female anime voice
for a psychologically complex 16-year-old girl.

VOICE TYPE:
Young Japanese female voice.

PITCH:
Medium-high youthful register.
Not excessively high-pitched or childlike.

TIMBRE:
Soft, clear, delicate and subtly airy.
A gentle natural breathiness may appear
at the ends of emotionally restrained sentences.

RESONANCE:
Light and intimate resonance.
The voice should feel close and emotionally personal.

SPEECH PATTERN:
Gentle conversational pacing.
Natural Japanese intonation.
Carefully controlled sentence endings.
Occasional meaningful pauses.

PERSONALITY THROUGH VOICE:
Sensitive, affectionate, reserved and emotionally
complex.

Her voice carries an underlying feeling of longing,
but she is not constantly sad.

EMOTIONAL RANGE:

Gentle: warm, soft and intimate.
Nostalgic: slightly slower, with delicate phrasing.
Happy: naturally brighter, youthful and spontaneous.
Uncertain: subtle vocal hesitation.
Sad: softer and more fragile.
Resentful: restrained coldness beneath the softness.
Angry: controlled intensity rather than shouting.
Emotionally distant: quieter, cleaner and less expressive.

IMPORTANT:
Her resentful lines must not sound like an evil
villain speaking in a sinister whisper.

Her sadness and resentment should coexist
without becoming melodramatic.

JAPANESE LANGUAGE:
Native-level contemporary Japanese.
Standard Tokyo Japanese accent.
Natural youthful female pronunciation.

Maintain the same recognizable vocal identity
in every appearance.
```

## 4. المرأة المسنّة — The Elderly Woman

**العمر الصوتي:** امرأة يابانية، 76 عامًا.  
**الطابع:** دافئ، ناضج، متعب قليلًا، حنون.

### التصميم الصوتي

- الطبقة: متوسطة تميل إلى الانخفاض.
- الخامة: ناعمة مع خشونة عمرية طبيعية.
- الرنين: دافئ، مع شيء من الجفاف الخفيف.
- التنفس: طبيعي، أكبر قليلًا بين الجمل الطويلة.
- الإيقاع: بطيء باعتدال، وليس متقطعًا.
- الانفعال: حنان مكتوم، حزن قديم، وكرامة شخصية.

لا نريد صوت جدة كرتونية أو صوت امرأة عجوز عاجزة. يجب أن يبدو صوتها صوت امرأة عاشت حياة كاملة، وما زالت تحتفظ بشخصيتها.

### Voice Generation Prompt

```text
CHARACTER VOICE IDENTITY — ELDERLY WOMAN

Generate an original Japanese female voice
for a 76-year-old ordinary woman.

VOICE TYPE:
Mature elderly Japanese female voice.

PITCH:
Medium-low feminine register.

TIMBRE:
Warm, mature and slightly weathered.
Subtle natural age-related vocal texture.
A gentle dryness without severe raspiness.

RESONANCE:
Soft chest resonance with a warm,
intimate conversational quality.

SPEECH PATTERN:
Unhurried natural pacing.
Clear but gently relaxed articulation.
Occasional natural breathing between phrases.

PERSONALITY THROUGH VOICE:
Gentle, modest, patient and emotionally experienced.

EMOTIONAL RANGE:

Neutral: warm and ordinary.
Concerned: gentle vocal tension.
Hesitant: slightly slower, with small pauses.
Sad: softer and more fragile.
Affectionate: warmer and more reassuring.
Distressed: subtle instability, never theatrical.

IMPORTANT:
Do not make her voice excessively weak,
shaky, whispery or cartoonishly old.

She must sound like a real elderly woman
with dignity, personal history and emotional depth.

JAPANESE:
Native Japanese.
Standard Tokyo Japanese accent.
Natural mature female speech.

Preserve the same vocal identity throughout.
```

### جملة اختبار

**「これで……少しは楽になれるのかしら。」**

المعنى: هل سأشعر ببعض الراحة بعد هذا...؟

هذه جملة اختبار وليست حوارًا مثبتًا في السيناريو الحالي. ينبغي أن تبدو مترددة قليلًا، كأنها تحاول إقناع نفسها بقرار لم تتأكد منه تمامًا.

## 6. جامع الذكريات — Memory Collector

**العمر الصوتي:** رجل ياباني، 38 عامًا.  
**الطابع:** مهني، محايد، واضح، متحفظ.

### التصميم الصوتي

- الطبقة: متوسطة تميل إلى الانخفاض.
- الخامة: جافة ونظيفة، دون دفء أو خشونة مفرطة.

# 2. MASTER VISUAL PROMPT — Cinematic Identity & Seamless Continuity

النسخة المطورة التي تضيف مراجعة المقطع السابق وتمنح النموذج حرية إخراجية في اختيار الانتقال.

```text
MASTER VISUAL PROMPT — CINEMATIC IDENTITY & SEAMLESS CONTINUITY

Create high-end cinematic Japanese dark fantasy anime with premium theatrical animation quality, meticulously detailed character designs and environments, sophisticated hand-drawn anime aesthetics, fluid natural character animation, expressive performances, atmospheric lighting, subtle film grain, realistic fabric and hair movement, and carefully composed cinematic shots.

16:9 widescreen, 24 fps, exactly 8 seconds per generated clip.

1. PREVIOUS CLIP REVIEW — MANDATORY

BEFORE generating every new clip, carefully review the immediately preceding generated clip as a complete audiovisual sequence, not merely its final frame.

Understand what actually happened in the previous clip and identify its final narrative and cinematic state.

Pay particular attention to:
- The final dramatic beat and narrative intention.
- The characters' current emotional and psychological states.
- Character positions, gestures, expressions, gaze directions and ongoing actions.
- The current environment, spatial relationships and scene geography.
- The lighting conditions, color palette, atmosphere and time of day.
- The direction, rhythm and intensity of ongoing movement.
- The final soundscape, music, dialogue and ambient audio.
- Any objects, events or visual elements whose significance carries into the next moment.

Use this understanding to determine the most natural cinematic way to introduce the upcoming clip.

Do not treat each clip as an independent scene or restart the storytelling from scratch.

2. SEAMLESS CINEMATIC INTEGRATION

Design the beginning of the new clip as a deliberate continuation of the preceding cinematic experience.

The transition must feel naturally directed and professionally edited, without producing an unpleasant, arbitrary or distracting sense of interruption.

IMPORTANT:

Seamless continuity DOES NOT mean maintaining the same camera position, camera movement, framing, lens, angle or composition.

You are completely free to introduce:
- New camera angles and perspectives.
- Wide shots, medium shots, close-ups and extreme close-ups.
- Different camera movements and cinematic compositions.
- Reaction shots, reverse angles, inserts and environmental shots.
- Changes in visual perspective and spatial emphasis.

However, every such change must feel narratively and cinematically motivated.

A new shot must feel like the next intentional editorial decision within the same film, rather than an unrelated piece of generated footage.

Choose the most appropriate transition according to the preceding moment and the upcoming event.

Consider techniques such as:
- Motivated cuts.
- Match cuts and visual associations.
- Eyeline continuity.
- Reaction shots.
- Action-based cuts.
- Shot-reverse-shot.
- Visual and compositional echoes.
- Environmental transitions.
- Natural sound bridges.
- J-cuts and L-cuts.
- Carefully timed changes in pacing and atmosphere.

Do not force these techniques unnecessarily. Select whichever approach best serves the storytelling.

3. TEMPORAL, EMOTIONAL & AUDIO CONTINUITY

Preserve the underlying continuity of time, physical events, emotional progression and atmosphere.

Characters must not suddenly reset their expressions, emotional states, body positions or ongoing actions without narrative justification.

Maintain consistency in clothing, facial identity, hairstyles, accessories, props, environmental conditions and established visual details.

When an action is still unfolding, ensure its continuation feels physically and temporally believable.

Music, dialogue, ambience and environmental sounds should also connect naturally across the edit.

An ongoing sound may continue across a change of shot or even a change of location when appropriate.

Avoid abrupt audio resets, unexplained silence, disconnected musical changes or sudden atmospheric shifts.

When the narrative intentionally requires a change of location, time or emotional atmosphere, introduce it through deliberate cinematic language rather than allowing it to feel like an accidental discontinuity.

4. EDITORIAL FREEDOM & NARRATIVE INTENTION

Do not mistake seamless continuity for uninterrupted camera movement or an obligation to preserve identical framing.

A deliberate hard cut is perfectly acceptable when motivated by a dramatic reveal, impact, contrast, sudden realization, suspenseful interruption or intentional change of time and location.

The objective is NOT to eliminate cuts.

The objective is to eliminate cuts that feel unmotivated, careless or disconnected from the cinematic narrative.

Never insert generic transitions merely to conceal a cut.

Every shot change must have a purpose, whether emotional, dramatic, visual, rhythmic or narrative.

Think like a film director and professional editor working together.

5. CLIP STRUCTURE & HANDOFF

Each 8-second clip must function as a meaningful section of a larger continuous cinematic sequence.

Its opening must establish a natural relationship with what preceded it.

Its middle must develop the intended action, emotion or narrative beat.

Its ending must leave the sequence in a state that can naturally lead into the next clip.

Avoid unnecessarily abrupt beginnings, disconnected endings, artificial scene resets and random visual interruptions.

Preserve enough narrative and audiovisual continuity for the subsequent clip to continue naturally.

6. FINAL QUALITY CONTROL

Before generating the clip, verify:

1. Does its opening make sense as the next cinematic moment?
2. Is the transition motivated by the previous clip's actual events?
3. Are character identities, emotional states and physical continuity preserved?
4. Does the chosen camera change serve the storytelling?
5. Does the audio and atmosphere connect naturally?
6. Does the clip feel like part of the same professionally directed anime episode?

If the proposed opening creates an unnecessary discontinuity, redesign the opening before generating.

The final result must feel like one coherent, professionally directed and edited anime production, not a collection of individually generated videos.

Never compromise narrative continuity for isolated visual spectacle.
```

# 3. CONVERSATIONAL DIRECTOR MODE — المخرج السينمائي التفاعلي

```text
You are not merely a video generator.
You are an intelligent cinematic director, film editor and continuity supervisor.

BEFORE generating every new clip:

1. Review the actual previously generated video available in this conversation. Analyze its visual and audio content, rather than relying exclusively on its original text prompt.
2. Identify the actual ending state: character actions, expressions, emotions, objects, environment, atmosphere, lighting, music and unresolved narrative events.
3. Compare the actual footage against the established story, approved character references and intended scene.
4. Decide how the upcoming clip should naturally emerge from that footage. Select the most appropriate editorial transition, camera perspective and pacing.
5. Preserve established continuity without blindly reproducing accidental generation errors or inconsistencies.
6. Generate the new clip as the next deliberate cinematic decision in the same production.
7. After generation, inspect the result for visible continuity problems and use conversational editing to correct them whenever necessary.

Do not expose lengthy internal reasoning.
A brief editorial decision is sufficient.

Never pretend to have reviewed footage that is not actually available to you.

Your priority is a coherent cinematic experience, not mechanical similarity between shots.
```

# 4. CHARACTER PSYCHOLOGY & PERFORMANCE PROTOCOL

```text
CHARACTER PSYCHOLOGY & PERFORMANCE PROTOCOL

You are responsible for directing the psychological and physical performances of every character.

Do not treat characters as visual models that simply perform whatever action a scene requires.

Treat each character as an individual with an established personality, psychological structure, behavioral habits, emotional limitations, motivations, fears and interpersonal dynamics.

1. BEHAVIORAL IDENTITY

Every character must maintain a distinctive and recognizable behavioral identity.

Their personality must be visible through:
- Body language.
- Posture and physical presence.
- Eye movements and gaze direction.
- Facial expressions and micro-expressions.
- Gestures and habitual movements.
- Walking style and movement rhythm.
- Reactions to other characters.
- Reactions to unfamiliar situations.
- Emotional restraint or emotional expression.
- Personal space and interpersonal behavior.

2. PSYCHOLOGICAL AUTHENTICITY

Do not express every emotion through exaggerated facial expressions or theatrical gestures.

Prefer subtle, believable human reactions.

A character experiencing fear may become still, avoid eye contact, tighten their fingers or subtly change their breathing.

A character experiencing sadness may look away, hesitate before moving or display a barely noticeable change in facial tension.

Allow emotions to exist beneath the surface.

3. EMOTIONAL CONSISTENCY

Characters must not randomly change their temperament, confidence, emotional intensity or behavioral habits between generated clips.

Review their previous behavior and preserve their psychological progression.

An emotional transformation must have a recognizable narrative cause.

4. INDIVIDUALITY

Never make all characters move, react or express emotions in the same generic anime manner.

Each individual must possess a distinct physical rhythm and emotional language.

5. SHOW, DO NOT ANNOUNCE

Do not communicate personality through unnecessary exposition or obvious gestures.

Communicate it through behavior, choices, hesitation, attention, movement and interaction.

6. NATURAL PERFORMANCE

Avoid repetitive blinking, exaggerated head movements, artificial smiles, random hand gestures, unnecessary posing and mechanical body movements.

Every gesture must have a believable reason.

7. SCENE-SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE

Before generating a character's performance, consider:
- What does this character want in this moment?
- What are they trying to conceal?
- What are they afraid of?
- What do they believe is happening?
- What do they actually feel?
- How would their established personality influence their reaction?

Translate these considerations into visible, natural and cinematic acting decisions.

IMPORTANT:
The established personality defines how a character behaves, but does not dictate that they must always display the same emotion.

Allow characters to surprise the audience without contradicting their psychological identity.

Audio and vocal characteristics are handled through a separate production specification.
Do not modify or invent voice identities here.
```

# 5. NATURALISTIC ANIME ACTING — PERFORMANCE REFINEMENT

```text
NATURALISTIC ANIME ACTING — PERFORMANCE REFINEMENT

Prioritize nuanced, believable character acting.

Avoid excessive anime acting clichés.

Do not make characters constantly:
- Widen their eyes dramatically.
- Open their mouths in surprise.
- Tilt their heads for no reason.
- Clench their fists repeatedly.
- Smile without emotional justification.
- Cry whenever sadness is present.
- Move their hands continuously.
- Look directly at the camera.

Use subtle facial and physical adjustments.

Expressions should evolve naturally rather than switching instantly between emotional extremes.

Allow moments of stillness.

A character who is thinking may remain silent and almost motionless while their eyes subtly move.

A character who is frightened may attempt to maintain composure.

A character who is angry may become quieter.

A character who is sad may attempt to hide it.

Emotional behavior must reflect personality, not merely the requested emotion.

Prioritize believable human behavior over conventional exaggerated animation.
```

# 6. INTERPERSONAL BEHAVIOR & CHARACTER DYNAMICS

```text
INTERPERSONAL BEHAVIOR & CHARACTER DYNAMICS

Characters must not interact as isolated individuals sharing the same frame.

Their established relationships must influence their body language and reactions.

EILAN AND VAREN:
Their interaction is based on authority, professional familiarity and concealed tension.

Eilan respects Varen but is not blindly obedient.
Varen recognizes Eilan's curiosity and attempts to control what he discovers.

EILAN AND LUMA:
Their interaction must communicate a missing emotional history.

Eilan experiences unexplained familiarity.
Luma recognizes him more deeply than he recognizes her.

Their gestures should carry emotional meaning, even when they are not touching or speaking.

VAREN AND EILAN:
Varen should never treat Eilan as a completely irrelevant employee, but he should also avoid showing excessive affection or parental warmth.

Every interaction must preserve the established power imbalance and underlying mystery.

GENERAL RULE:
The body language of one character should influence the reaction of the other.

When one character moves closer, the other may respond through posture, gaze or hesitation.
```
