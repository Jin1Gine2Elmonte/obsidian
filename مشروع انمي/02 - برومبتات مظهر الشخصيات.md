# الشخصية الرئيسية

## 1. إيلان سار — Eilan Sar

**الهوية البصرية:** شاب يبدو هادئًا من الخارج، لكن ملامحه تحمل إرهاقًا داخليًا خفيًا. لا ينبغي أن يبدو كبطل أنمي تقليدي مبالغ في وسامته أو قوته.

### Character Generation Prompt

```text
Create a highly detailed professional anime character
reference design for EILAN SAR.

IDENTITY:
A 19-year-old young man, human, with a slender
but naturally athletic physique. Approximately
178 cm tall. His proportions are realistic and
mature, with slightly narrow shoulders and
long natural limbs.

FACE:
A refined but somewhat tired youthful face.
Subtle cheekbones, a defined but not overly
sharp jawline, straight nose and naturally
proportioned lips.

His expression is usually reserved, observant
and emotionally restrained.

EYES:
Cool ash-gray eyes with subtle silver reflections.
His gaze is intelligent, analytical and occasionally
distant, as though something is constantly
occupying his thoughts.

HAIR:
Medium-length messy black hair.
Natural layered strands, slightly uneven bangs,
soft volume and realistic individual hair strands.

One distinctive silver-white streak near his
left temple. This feature is essential and must
never disappear or change position.

CLOTHING:
A long dark midnight-navy gothic coat extending
to approximately the knees.
Elegant structured collar, subtle shoulder tailoring,
dark fabric with realistic folds and texture.

Several small aged copper buttons running
vertically down the front.

Underneath, a simple dark charcoal shirt,
dark fitted trousers and black leather boots.

ACCESSORY:
A thin, visibly worn red thread bracelet around
his wrist. It is a permanent identifying feature.

PERSONALITY THROUGH APPEARANCE:
Quiet, introspective, observant and emotionally
guarded. He does not constantly look angry.
His emotions are communicated through subtle
eye movements, slight tension around the mouth
and restrained body language.

VISUAL STYLE:
Premium cinematic Japanese dark fantasy anime.
Sophisticated character illustration, precise anatomy,
beautiful facial rendering and detailed clothing.

Create a complete character reference sheet:
front view, side profile, back view,
full-body standing pose and facial expressions.

Include neutral, confused, frightened, sorrowful
and determined expressions.

White or neutral studio background.

STRICT CONSISTENCY:
Never change his face, age, hairstyle, silver streak,
eye color, clothing design, bracelet or body proportions.
```

## 2. لوما سار — Luma Sar

**الهوية البصرية:** فتاة تبدو وكأنها تنتمي إلى عالم آخر. جمالها هادئ، وليس استعراضيًا. يجب أن تحمل ملامحها شيئًا مألوفًا لإيلان، وفي الوقت نفسه شيئًا غير مريح للمشاهد.

### Character Generation Prompt

```text
Create a highly detailed professional anime character
reference design for LUMA SAR.

IDENTITY:
A mysterious 16-year-old girl with a delicate,
slender natural physique and youthful proportions.

FACE:
Soft youthful facial features, delicate cheeks,
a small natural nose and subtle lips.

Her beauty should feel understated, melancholic
and strangely memorable.

EYES:
Her most distinctive feature is her heterochromia:
one luminous golden-amber eye and one pale
silver-gray eye.

Both eyes are highly expressive.

Her gaze combines familiarity, sadness and
an unsettling sense of awareness.

HAIR:
Short silver-white hair in a naturally layered
bob haircut, ending around the jawline.

Soft uneven bangs frame her forehead.
Fine individual strands catch the surrounding light.

Her hair must remain silver-white in every scene.

CLOTHING:
A modest, elegant dark gothic dress.
Deep charcoal and muted midnight-blue fabrics.
Long sleeves, delicate layered fabric and subtle
antique silver detailing.

The dress should look slightly old-fashioned,
as though it belongs to another era.

ACCESSORY:
A small blue translucent glass pendant suspended
from a thin dark necklace.

The pendant emits an extremely subtle blue glow
under certain lighting conditions.

PERSONALITY THROUGH APPEARANCE:
Quiet, emotionally complex and mysterious.

She rarely displays exaggerated expressions.
Her sadness is often communicated through her eyes,
small movements and prolonged silence.

Her smile should feel delicate and slightly painful.

VISUAL STYLE:
Premium Japanese dark fantasy anime,
cinematic character design, subtle ethereal lighting,
beautifully rendered silver hair and expressive eyes.

Create a full character reference sheet:
front view, side view, back view, full-body pose
and multiple facial expressions.

STRICT CONSISTENCY:
Preserve her exact age, facial structure,
silver hairstyle, heterochromatic eyes,
dark gothic dress and blue glass pendant.

Never redesign her between scenes.
```

## 3. فارِن — Varen

**الهوية البصرية:** رجل لا يحتاج إلى رفع صوته لفرض سلطته. مظهره أنيق، لكنه يحمل آثار عمر طويل من التعامل مع أشياء لا ينبغي للإنسان رؤيتها.

### Character Generation Prompt

```text
Create a highly detailed professional anime character
reference design for VAREN.

IDENTITY:
A 54-year-old male senior archivist.
Approximately 183 cm tall.

He has a slender, upright and dignified physique.
His posture is naturally disciplined and controlled.

FACE:
A mature, angular face with visible age lines.
Defined cheekbones, a slightly narrow jaw,
subtle wrinkles around the eyes and forehead.

His appearance conveys intelligence, experience
and emotional restraint.

EYES:
His right eye is completely blind, with a pale,
cloudy iris and a slightly faded appearance.

His left eye is dark gray, sharp and observant.

HAIR:
Silver-gray hair, neatly arranged backward
with a few natural loose strands.

His hairstyle is elegant, mature and practical.

CLOTHING:
A formal black gothic archivist uniform.
Long structured black coat with a high collar,
subtle antique silver fastenings and refined tailoring.

Dark inner shirt, black trousers and polished
black formal boots.

ACCESSORIES:
Always wears fitted black gloves.

No unnecessary jewelry or decorative accessories.

PERSONALITY THROUGH APPEARANCE:
Authoritative, calculating, composed and difficult
to read.

His expressions are restrained.
He rarely raises his eyebrows or displays obvious fear.

When genuinely frightened, the change should be
subtle but unmistakable.

VISUAL STYLE:
High-end Japanese dark fantasy anime,
cinematic lighting, realistic aged facial details,
detailed black fabric and refined character design.

Generate front, side, back and full-body views,
along with neutral, stern, suspicious and frightened
facial expressions.

STRICT CONSISTENCY:
Never change his age, facial structure,
silver-gray hairstyle, blind right eye,
dark-gray left eye, black uniform or gloves.
```

## 5. المرأة المسنّة — The Elderly Woman

**التصور البصري:** امرأة في السادسة والسبعين، ملامحها تحمل سنوات طويلة من التعب، لكنها تحتفظ بشيء من الرقة والكرامة. لا نريدها عجوزًا هشة بصورة مبالغ فيها، بل امرأة حقيقية عاشت حياة طويلة.

### Character Generation Prompt

```text
Create a highly detailed cinematic Japanese dark
fantasy anime character design.

CHARACTER: THE ELDERLY WOMAN

AGE: 76 years old.

PHYSICAL APPEARANCE:

An elderly human woman with a short, slightly
stooped but dignified posture.

She has a naturally aged face with pronounced
facial wrinkles, delicate crow's feet around
her eyes, subtle forehead lines and realistic
skin texture.

Her facial features are soft but visibly weathered
by age and a difficult life.

Her complexion is pale with a slightly muted,
natural skin tone.

Her eyes are faded warm brown, slightly sunken
but still expressive and attentive.

HAIR:

Long silver-gray hair, loosely gathered into
a slightly untidy low bun behind her head.

A few thin strands escape around her temples
and forehead.

Her hair is fine, naturally aged and slightly
wispy, with realistic individual strands.

CLOTHING:

A simple, worn charcoal-gray wool shawl
covering her shoulders.

Underneath, a modest dark burgundy-brown dress
made from old, coarse fabric.

Long sleeves, a high modest neckline,
slightly worn cuffs and visible fabric aging.

Her clothing should communicate an ordinary
working-class citizen of an old gothic city.

ACCESSORIES:

Small, simple wooden earrings.
No expensive jewelry.

BODY DETAILS:

Thin aged hands with visible veins,
natural age spots and slightly bent fingers.

OVERALL IMPRESSION:

A dignified elderly woman whose appearance
communicates a long and emotionally complicated life.

Her design must feel grounded, human and believable.

STYLE:

Premium theatrical Japanese dark fantasy anime.
Highly detailed facial anatomy, sophisticated
hand-drawn aesthetic, cinematic character rendering,
beautifully textured clothing and natural proportions.

Create a single-character reference portrait
with a clearly visible full-body design and
a complementary facial close-up.

Neutral muted background.

Do not include other characters.
No text, labels or watermarks.
```

## 7. جامع الذكريات — The Memory Collector

**التصور البصري:** موظف محترف يعمل في مؤسسة رسمية. يجب أن يبدو زيه منظمًا واحترافيًا، لكن تفاصيله تحمل شيئًا غير مألوف. قناع فضي يغطي نصف الوجه، لا قناع كامل، حتى تبقى العينان والفم قادرين على التعبير.

### Character Generation Prompt

```text
Create an exceptionally detailed cinematic Japanese
dark fantasy anime character design.

CHARACTER: THE MEMORY COLLECTOR

AGE: Approximately 38 years old.

GENDER: Male.

PHYSICAL APPEARANCE:

An average-height adult man, approximately 179 cm.

He has a lean, professionally conditioned physique.

His posture is upright and controlled.

His facial structure is mature and slightly angular,
with a narrow jawline and subtle cheekbones.

SKIN:

A pale, slightly desaturated complexion.

No exaggerated scars or monstrous features.

HAIR:

Short, neatly combed dark ash-brown hair.

The hairstyle is practical, with a subtle side part.

No extravagant styling.

EYES:

Cold muted gray eyes.

His gaze is focused, professional and observant.

His eyes must remain visible and expressive.

MASK:

A distinctive antique silver half-mask covering
the upper central portion of his face.

The mask extends around the bridge of the nose
and partially around the cheekbones, while leaving
his mouth and lower jaw visible.

Its surface contains delicate engraved geometric
lines inspired by ancient archival instruments.

The mask is elegant, institutional and slightly
unsettling.

Avoid skull motifs, monstrous shapes or exaggerated
fantasy ornamentation.

CLOTHING:

A long black institutional uniform extending
to the lower calves.

Structured shoulders, a high collar and fitted sleeves.

The primary material is matte black heavy fabric.

Very subtle silver metallic stitching follows
selected seams.

A narrow dark gray belt holds small professional
tools and a glass vial container.

His outfit resembles a combination of an old
medical technician's uniform and a gothic
institutional official's clothing.

GLOVES:

Fitted black leather gloves with subtle reinforced
fingertips.

EQUIPMENT:

A slim silver extraction instrument, approximately
the length of a medical pen.

A small transparent cylindrical memory vial
inside a protective metal holder.

Both objects must appear carefully manufactured
and consistent with the archive's technology.

SILHOUETTE:

Long, clean and disciplined.

No excessive armor, capes or decorative accessories.

OVERALL IMPRESSION:

A professional government employee who performs
an extraordinary and morally disturbing procedure
as part of his ordinary occupation.

His appearance should be unsettling through
precision and restraint rather than horror imagery.

STYLE:

Premium theatrical Japanese dark fantasy anime,
intricate costume design, realistic metallic
surfaces, detailed fabric textures and sophisticated
character rendering.

Create an isolated single-character full-body design
with a detailed facial close-up.

Neutral background.

No other people, text or watermarks.
```

# 14. برومبت ورقة النمذجة الموحدة — Character Model Sheet

أضف التعليمات التالية بعد برومبت أي شخصية عند توليد مرجعها:

```text
CHARACTER MODEL SHEET REQUIREMENTS:

Generate a professional animation production
character model sheet, not a cinematic scene.

The same character must be shown consistently
from multiple angles:

1. Front-facing full-body view.
2. Three-quarter view.
3. Side profile.
4. Back view.
5. Close-up facial portrait.
6. Five distinct facial expressions.

Maintain identical facial proportions,
hairstyle, eye color, clothing, accessories,
body proportions and identifying characteristics
across all views.

Use clean professional character concept art
presentation, neutral studio lighting,
clear anatomy and uncluttered background.

Do not create multiple different interpretations
of the same character.

Every view must depict exactly the same individual.
```

# 15. الهوية الفنية الموحدة — Unified Character Art Direction

أضف هذا الجزء إلى برومبتات الشخصيات الرئيسية والثانوية لضمان انتمائها إلى إنتاج أنمي واحد:

```text
UNIFIED CHARACTER ART DIRECTION:

All characters belong to the same dark fantasy
anime production: THE ARCHIVE OF ABSENCE.

Maintain a unified artistic direction across
every character design.

VISUAL LANGUAGE:

Premium Japanese theatrical dark fantasy anime.

Sophisticated hand-drawn character aesthetics.

Detailed but natural facial anatomy.

Expressive and beautifully rendered eyes.

Individual hair strands and carefully constructed
hairstyles.

Physically believable clothing folds and materials.

Subtle cinematic lighting.

Mature character designs without excessive
beautification or exaggerated proportions.

COLOR PHILOSOPHY:

Muted and atmospheric colors.

Charcoal, silver, midnight navy, dark brown,
deep burgundy and restrained copper.

Do not introduce bright saturated colors
without narrative justification.

CONSISTENCY:

All characters must look as though they belong
to the same anime, share the same world,
and were designed by the same professional
character design department.

Never independently change the art style,
facial rendering or material treatment
between different character designs.
```

## ملاحظة إنتاجية

- لا تدمج الشخصيات في صورة واحدة عند إنشاء مراجعها الأساسية.
- أنشئ صورة مستقلة لكل شخصية.
- احتفظ بمرجع مستقل للطفولة لكل من إيلان ولوما.
- يجب إرفاق مرجع الشخصية عند ظهورها في المقاطع.
- مظهر الشخصية لا يحدده برومبت المشهد وحده؛ المرجع البصري المعتمد هو مصدر الهوية.
