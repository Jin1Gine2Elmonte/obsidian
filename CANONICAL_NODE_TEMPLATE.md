## State
### CANONICAL / STRONGLY_SUPPORTED
Facts established or strongly supported by the available evidence.

### WORKING
Current interpretation that may still evolve.

### DERIVED
Analytical implications inferred from established material.

### SPECULATIVE
Possibilities that have not been confirmed.

### HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED
A prior state retained because it explains project or knowledge evolution but is not current.

### UNKNOWN
Insufficient evidence. Do not fill the gap by invention.

## Evidence

## Open questions

## Next evolution
