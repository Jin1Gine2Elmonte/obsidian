# High-Impact Recovery Matrix

## Purpose
Rank missing historical information by downstream impact rather than by curiosity. This is the order in which future conversation recovery should be attempted.

## Impact scoring
Each unresolved item is scored conceptually by:
- number of dependent characters;
- number of dependent world systems;
- amount of chronology affected;
- number of version boundaries affected;
- ability to change existing canon;
- ability to resolve other unknowns.

## Tier Ω — world-restructuring
### Ω1 — Jin ↔ Ryota identity
Potentially changes the Almont family, childhood chronology, Jin's biography, and the human/cosmic bridge.

### Ω2 — complete Jin variant genealogy
Potentially changes the interpretation of every existing Jin statement.

### Ω3 — exact Almont/child chronology
Potentially establishes which character belongs to which project/version and when the imperial branch intersects the broader universe.

### Ω4 — Arisa death scene
Potentially changes Almont, Ryota/Jin, the family before/after rupture, and the cause of the escape.

## Tier α — relational restructuring
### α1 — Adam's first meeting with Jin
May establish loyalty, world-travel origin, variant recognition, and continuity across worlds.

### α2 — Garthin's first defining scenes
May establish his real psychology and any direct relationship to the larger world.

### α3 — Benjamin's actual influence
May alter the political interpretation of Almont and the court.

## Tier β — rule restructuring
- exact reality-control limits;
- Magic boundaries;
- inherited-Power rules;
- spiritual-vessel mechanics;
- interworld travel rules;
- Dark Forest resistance mechanism.

These can prevent future power escalation and explain political institutions.

## Tier γ — institutional detail
- city numbering;
- capital structure;
- military hierarchy;
- communication;
- supply;
- succession;
- local governance.

Important for coherence but unlikely to resolve identity conflicts by itself.

## Tier δ — cosmological mapping
- First Creation;
- Primordial Rift;
- Existence Sublime;
- Entity of Forgetting hierarchy;
- Shadows;
- Corruption;
- Curse of the Ancients.

Deeply important, but should not be used to distract from Tier Ω identity gaps.

## Tier ε — visual/detail enrichment
Symbols, architecture, costumes, minor characters, city aesthetics, and descriptive details.

These are valuable after structural uncertainties are stabilized.

## Recovery rule
A newly recovered fact should be evaluated by what it changes. If one scene resolves three Tier Ω/α questions, its memory value is exceptionally high.

## Anti-bias rule
Do not prioritize the most mysterious concept simply because it is aesthetically exciting. Prioritize the evidence with the largest structural effect.
