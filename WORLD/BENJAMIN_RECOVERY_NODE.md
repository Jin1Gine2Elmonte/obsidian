# Benjamin — Recovery Node

## Current memory
Benjamin is remembered as an adviser to Emperor Almont. His role is confirmed at the broad level, while most of his individual characterization remains unrecovered.

## Why Benjamin matters
Advisers can be structurally invisible if stored only as a name. Benjamin should instead be recovered as a potential mechanism connecting:
- Almont's private will;
- imperial policy;
- court information;
- succession;
- family conflict;
- knowledge of Arisa's death;
- responses to Ryota's rebellion.

These are possible functions, not current canon.

## Recovery questions
- What does Benjamin want independently of Almont?
- Is he loyal to Almont, the empire, himself, or an ideology?
- Does he manipulate decisions or merely advise?
- Does he know the family's private history?
- What does he know about Ryota that Almont does not?
- What did he know before Arisa's death?
- What was his role afterward?
- How does he behave when Almont is absent?
- Does he fear Almont?
- Can he disagree openly?

## Scene priority
The highest-value material is not a biography paragraph. It is a scene in which Benjamin advises Almont under pressure, especially when the private family interest conflicts with imperial stability.

## Canon boundary
Do not infer Benjamin's motives from the word "adviser." Recover them from dialogue, action, and consequences.
