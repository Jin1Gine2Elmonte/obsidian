# World Recovery Matrix

This matrix is the next-pass map for importing historical conversations or documents. It prioritizes information by how much it would change understanding of the world.

## Priority A — identity and causality
- complete Jin version tree;
- precise relationship between Jin and Ryota naming/iterations;
- Almont/Ryota/Jin chronology;
- Arisa death scene and aftermath;
- exact reasons for departure/return cycles;
- Garthin's defining scenes;
- Adam's first meeting with Jin.

## Priority B — rules
- Magic definition and limits;
- inherited Powers rules;
- spiritual vessel mechanics;
- interworld travel mechanics;
- reality control limits;
- cosmological hierarchy.

## Priority C — world machinery
- imperial administration;
- military city numbering;
- capital/court;
- Dark Forest structure;
- political factions;
- bloodline institutions.

## Priority D — deep mythology
- First Creation;
- Primordial Rift;
- Existence Sublime;
- Entity of Forgetting;
- Shadows;
- Corruption;
- Curse of the Ancients.

## Priority E — atmosphere / visual canon
- visual designs;
- symbols;
- architecture;
- costumes;
- city aesthetics;
- recurring imagery;
- cover references.

## Evidence handling
A high-priority unknown should remain explicitly unknown until historical evidence is recovered. Do not fill Priority A gaps with Priority D speculation merely because the latter is easier to invent.

## Success condition
The goal is not to have a larger lore document. The goal is to be able to answer, for every major event:
**who acted, why, under what rule, what changed, what consequence followed, and which version of the world it belongs to.**
