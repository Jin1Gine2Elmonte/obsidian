# Arisa — Recovery Node

## Current memory
Arisa is remembered as Almont's wife and as a central figure in the family branch involving Ryota and/or the child-history branch currently under Jin/Ryota identity audit. Her death is a pivotal remembered rupture.

## What is actually known
- She is Almont's wife.
- She is the mother figure in the imperial family branch associated with Ryota.
- Her death is witnessed in the remembered child-history thread.
- The event is followed by escape and later return to the capital.

## What is missing
The archive currently lacks enough source-level detail to define:
- her personality;
- worldview;
- relationship with Almont before the death;
- relationship with the child/son;
- political position;
- exact circumstances of death;
- motive behind the death;
- perpetrator;
- location;
- immediate response of Almont;
- immediate response of the child;
- court response;
- long-term imperial consequences.

## Why this node has high priority
It is tempting to treat Arisa merely as the cause of a trauma arc. That would erase the person who existed before the rupture. Recovery must reconstruct her living role first, then the meaning of her death.

## Evidence targets
Search historical material for scenes where Arisa:
- speaks privately with Almont;
- speaks with her child;
- influences policy;
- expresses fear or desire;
- protects or challenges someone;
- discusses the capital;
- interacts with Benjamin;
- responds to the imperial system.

## Canon protection
Do not infer personality from her death alone. The archive needs evidence of who she was while alive.
