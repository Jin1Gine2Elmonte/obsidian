# Dark Forest — Memory-Density Extraction

## Confirmed memory
The Dark Forest is a dangerous, mysterious region not controlled by the empire. The empire otherwise controls most of the world in the remembered setting.

## Structural role
The forest is more than a location. It is a boundary around imperial totality: a place where centralized authority, knowledge, mapping, security, and predictability fail or become incomplete.

## Three kinds of boundary
### Geographic
The empire cannot fully occupy or govern the territory.

### Epistemic
The forest is mysterious; what is unknown inside it matters as much as what is physically inaccessible.

### Political
The forest demonstrates that imperial sovereignty has limits. That can affect propaganda, military doctrine, frontier policy, and the self-image of the empire.

These consequences are analytical rather than established details.

## Character function
A journey into or around the Dark Forest can expose characters to a world that is not organized according to imperial assumptions. For a character connected to the capital, leaving civilization can therefore become more than geographical escape.

## Recovery targets
- exact geography;
- size;
- known settlements;
- creatures/factions;
- why the empire cannot control it;
- what the empire believes about it;
- what is actually true;
- relation to magic or powers;
- relation to Jin or other cosmological anchors;
- known scenes occurring there.

## Canon protection
Do not turn the forest into a generic "mysterious evil forest". Its strongest remembered function is specifically as the limit of imperial control and knowledge.
