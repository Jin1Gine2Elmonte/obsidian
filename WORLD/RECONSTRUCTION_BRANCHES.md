# World Reconstruction Branches

## Branch 01 — Jin core
Known: Jin/Gin/Gene, fictional awareness, imagination/reality premise, extraordinary reality control.

## Branch 02 — Jin variants
Known: multiple versions/iterations discussed; complete catalog unavailable.

## Branch 03 — Almont family
Known: Almont emperor, Arisa wife, Ryota son, Benjamin adviser; Arisa death; rebellion/departure/return pattern.

## Branch 04 — Jin child-history / identity overlap
Known: a child/young Jin is also preserved in a family-history summary with Arisa's death, escape, capital return, rebellion, and repeated departures. Relationship to Ryota remains unresolved.

## Branch 05 — Empire
Known: broad world control, about 1,060 military cities, extreme city population scale, capital gravity, Dark Forest exception.

## Branch 06 — Garthin
Known: ruler, brutality, hidden spark of kindness, contradiction-first characterization.

## Branch 07 — Adam
Known: interworld traveler and Jin follower.

## Branch 08 — Power system
Known: Magic versus inherited Powers, spiritual-vessel concept.

## Branch 09 — Cosmology
Known anchors: Existence Sublime, Entity of Forgetting, Primordial Rift, Shadows of the First Creation, Abyss, Corruption, Curse of the Ancients, Blood family, Dreams of the Inverted Universe.

## Branch interaction
The archive should seek evidence at branch intersections, because important world truth is likely stored there:
- Jin ↔ Almont
- Jin ↔ Ryota
- Jin ↔ Adam
- Jin ↔ cosmology
- Almont ↔ empire
- Almont ↔ power inheritance
- Ryota ↔ succession
- Dark Forest ↔ imperial limits
- Garthin ↔ broader ruler archetypes

## Branch rule
A branch can share concepts with another branch without sharing identities. Shared names/themes are not enough to merge branches.
