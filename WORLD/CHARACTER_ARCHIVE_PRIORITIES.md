# Character Archive — Recovery Priorities

This index defines where the remaining depth of the Jin/Almont world is most likely to be found.

## Tier 1 — highest impact
1. Jin and all variants.
2. Almont.
3. Ryota / the father-son rupture.
4. Arisa and the circumstances surrounding her death.
5. Garthin.
6. Adam.

## Tier 2 — structural support
7. Benjamin.
8. Dark Forest inhabitants/groups.
9. imperial governors / military leadership.
10. bloodline holders of major Powers.

## For every character
Recover, when available:
- exact name/spelling;
- aliases;
- age at each major event;
- physical description;
- voice/dialogue tendencies;
- fears;
- desires;
- contradictions;
- relationships;
- secrets;
- powers;
- limitations;
- formative events;
- important scenes;
- decisions;
- consequences;
- deaths/disappearances;
- later revisions;
- canon state;
- source conversation/date.

## Scene-first recovery
Descriptions alone are insufficient for psychologically complex characters. When historical conversations are imported, prefer the actual scenes that reveal behavior. A scene can establish information that a character summary accidentally erases: hesitation, contradiction, resentment, attachment, fear, or an unspoken motive.

## Version safety
Character names may recur across different world generations. Every recovered character note should carry a project/world/version identifier before relationships are merged.
