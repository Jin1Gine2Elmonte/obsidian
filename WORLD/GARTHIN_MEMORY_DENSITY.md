# Garthin — Memory-Density Extraction

## Core remembered identity
Garthin is a ruler-shaped character anchor defined by contradiction: he can be brutal and monstrous while carrying a hidden, difficult-to-understand spark of kindness that appears under extreme circumstances.

## Why the contradiction matters
The contradiction is not useful because it makes Garthin secretly good. Its value is that it prevents moral compression. A character can perform cruel acts without the narrative resolving him into a simple evil archetype, while a small act of tenderness can reveal an interior process without redeeming him.

## Representation rule
The user's preferred method is indirect revelation. Garthin's inner structure should be extracted from what he does when no reward exists, what he refuses to do, what he notices during violence, how he reacts to vulnerability, what changes around specific people, moments of involuntary care, silences, delays, and contradictions between public authority and private impulse.

## Ruler function
As a ruler, Garthin can be studied on two levels: public system — authority, violence, decisions, command; private organism — repression, attachment, fear, need, instinct, and hidden tenderness. The strongest version emerges when these levels interfere with one another.

## Unknown canon
Current accessible memory does not preserve enough scene-level detail to safely define exact territory, chronology, relationship to Almont, relationship to Jin, relationship to Adam, political structure, specific atrocities or mercies, origin, or ending. These are retrieval targets, not gaps to fill by invention.

## Recovery questions
1. Which scene first established Garthin's contradiction?
2. Who witnessed the hidden kindness?
3. Was the act intentional or involuntary?
4. What does Garthin fear losing?
5. Does he understand his own tenderness?
6. How does his public identity constrain private behavior?
7. Does power make his contradiction easier or harder to express?
8. Which characters provoke different versions of him?

## Cross-project relationship
Garthin connects naturally to the user's broader philosophy of treating actions as forces rather than moral labels. This is a conceptual relationship, not confirmed plot canon with Jin, Almont, Adam, or the cosmology.
