# Garthin — Relational Architecture

## Why Garthin matters
Garthin is preserved as a character whose main value is contradiction rather than a simple moral classification. His public role as ruler creates power and fear; his hidden flashes of kindness create evidence of an interior that cannot be reduced to the public role.

## Public / private interference
The strongest character model is not:

`public tyrant + secretly nice person`

but:

`public authority continuously shaping the conditions under which private impulses can or cannot appear.`

This distinction matters because a hidden kindness can itself be distorted by power, fear, pride, repression, or possession.

## Scene-level extraction
For every future Garthin scene, extract:
- who has power;
- who believes they have power;
- what Garthin wants;
- what he says;
- what he refuses to say;
- what he notices;
- what he protects;
- what he destroys;
- what action contradicts his stated position;
- whether tenderness is deliberate or involuntary;
- immediate consequence;
- later consequence.

## Character truth test
A useful scene should reveal something that a static adjective cannot reveal. "Cruel" is not enough. A specific choice under pressure is evidence.

## Relationship recovery priority
Recover scenes connecting Garthin to:
1. subjects;
2. subordinates;
3. enemies;
4. vulnerable people;
5. people he genuinely values;
6. Jin;
7. Adam;
8. Almont or the imperial system if the relationship is actually canonical.

The last two are retrieval targets, not current canon.

## Psychological questions
- What kind of vulnerability does Garthin tolerate?
- What kind does he punish?
- Is kindness a conscious choice, reflex, attachment, guilt, memory, or something else?
- Does he recognize the contradiction?
- Does he resent the part of himself that acts kindly?
- Does his authority make him more violent because softness is dangerous to display?

## Canon boundary
No answer to the psychological questions above should be promoted into canon without scene-level evidence.
