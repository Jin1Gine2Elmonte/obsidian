# Adam — Interworld Traveler

## Ownership
This is the **canonical identity owner** for Adam: durable identity and broad role only. Travel-mechanics hypotheses belong in `ADAM_INTERWORLD_MODEL.md`; memory completeness belongs in `ADAM_MEMORY_DENSITY.md`; Jin-version recognition belongs in `ADAM_JIN_VARIANT_DISCRIMINATION.md`.

## Core memory
Adam is remembered as a traveler between worlds and a follower/companion of Jin.

## Structural role
Adam provides a character-scale interface to a cosmology larger than any single world. His movement between worlds can connect otherwise isolated settings, but the current memory does not preserve the exact mechanics of travel.

## Relationship to Jin
The remembered relationship is follower + traveler/companion. It is durable that Adam accompanies or follows Jin across worlds; the deeper nature of that attachment—loyalty, dependence, disagreement, friendship, worship, fear, or ideological alignment—remains under-recovered.

Adam is potentially important for distinguishing Jin variants because a character who travels between worlds may encounter more than one manifestation/iteration of Jin. This is an investigation axis, not proof that Adam actually recognizes every version.

## What is durable
- He travels between worlds.
- He follows/accompanies Jin.
- His existence is potentially relevant to continuity across worlds.
- His interworld position makes him a useful candidate for future continuity/recollection analysis.

## What is missing
- origin;
- first meeting with Jin;
- independent motivation;
- personal goals apart from following Jin;
- explicit disagreements or loyalties;
- memory retention across world transitions;
- exact travel mechanics;
- knowledge of different Jin versions;
- reasons he continues following Jin;
- boundaries on where/when he can travel.

## Canon boundary
Do not invent a portal system, teleportation mechanism, detailed backstory, or specific cosmological office for Adam from the limited current memory.
