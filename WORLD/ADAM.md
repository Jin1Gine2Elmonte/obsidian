# Adam — Interworld Traveler

## Core memory
Adam is remembered as a traveler between worlds and a follower of Jin.

## Structural role
Adam provides a human/character-scale interface to a cosmology larger than any single world. His movement between worlds can connect otherwise isolated settings, but the current memory does not preserve the exact mechanics of travel.

## Relationship to Jin
The remembered relationship is follower + traveler. The depth of loyalty, dependence, disagreement, friendship, worship, fear, or ideological alignment is not sufficiently preserved.

## Questions to recover
- How did Adam first encounter Jin?
- Why does Adam follow him?
- Can Adam leave Jin voluntarily?
- Does Adam understand Jin's fictional/metaphysical status?
- How many worlds has Adam visited?
- What has Adam witnessed that makes him different?
- Does Adam have a home world?
- What powers or tools permit travel?
- What is the cost of interworld movement?
- Is Adam a bridge between storylines?
- Does Adam retain memory across world changes?

## Narrative value
An interworld traveler can carry information between otherwise isolated stories. The archive should watch for continuity facts Adam might preserve: names, events, cosmological rules, alternate versions, historical discrepancies, and changes between worlds.

## Canon discipline
Do not invent a portal system, teleportation mechanism, or specific cosmological role until recovered. The durable fact is that Adam travels between worlds and follows Jin.
