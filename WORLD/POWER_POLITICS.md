# Power ↔ Politics Extraction

## Established inputs
Magic is transcendent and difficult to learn. Powers are more logical/constrained and can be inherited through blood. A spiritual-vessel concept exists.

## Political consequences to recover
Because Powers can be inherited through blood, the world may tie ability to lineage. The historical material should be searched for explicit evidence involving:
- noble/family status;
- military recruitment;
- marriage and bloodline preservation;
- succession;
- fear of inherited abilities;
- discrimination;
- attempts to control or eliminate powerful bloodlines;
- legal distinctions between users and non-users;
- political legitimacy based on power.

## Military consequences
Search for scenes that demonstrate how Magic and Powers are actually valued by military institutions. A stated difference is less informative than a battle, training session, promotion, punishment, or strategic decision where the distinction matters.

## Spiritual vessel
The vessel could connect personal capacity to embodiment, health, inheritance, or failure, but none of those are confirmed. The exact mechanics must be recovered from explicit source material.

## Narrative principle
A power system becomes worldbuilding when institutions react to it. The archive should therefore capture not only what powers can do but what families, armies, rulers, and ordinary people do because those powers exist.
