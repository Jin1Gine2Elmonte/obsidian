# Causal Map — Jin / Almont / Empire / Family / Outer World

This is a relationship-and-causality model reconstructed only from durable context. It is not a completed plot synopsis.

## Central chain
**Almont's imperial authority** -> creates a family/political environment in which inheritance and position matter -> **Ryota/Jin remains tied to the capital despite conflict** -> **Arisa's death** becomes a rupture -> **escape** interrupts the family/political continuity -> **later return** reintroduces the character into the capital's power structure -> **continued rebellion** prevents the return from becoming reconciliation -> **repeated departures** turn geography into part of the character's resistance.

## Parallel boundary chain
**Empire controls most of the world** -> centralized authority becomes the dominant environmental condition -> **Dark Forest remains outside imperial control** -> reveals that imperial totality is incomplete -> creates a persistent external unknown.

## Power chain
**Inherited Powers** -> tie supernatural ability to blood/family -> connects power to lineage, inheritance, identity, and political status.

**Magic** -> remains a separate, less logical/more transcendent category -> prevents the world's supernatural order from becoming reducible to bloodline alone.

## Character contradiction chain
**Garthin's rulership** + **brutality** + **hidden kindness** -> produces moral/psychological contradiction -> supports the broader creative principle that characters should not collapse into good/evil labels.

## Cosmological chain
**Imagination as non-ordinary realm** -> enables fictional-awareness premise -> connects Jin to reality manipulation and meta-fictional structure -> opens the world toward Existence Sublime and broader cosmological anchors.

## Adam bridge
**Adam as interworld traveler** + **follower of Jin** -> creates a possible connective route between otherwise separate worlds. Exact travel mechanics and information carried across worlds remain unrecovered.

## Important non-causal relationships
Some nodes are related by theme without proof of direct plot causality. In particular, the Entity of Forgetting, Existence Sublime, Shadows of the First Creation, Strings of Fate, and Jin may coexist within the user's wider creative ecosystem without necessarily belonging to the same canonical storyline. Do not infer shared canon from thematic similarity.

## Recovery priority
For every recovered historical conversation, ask:
1. What changed?
2. What caused the change?
3. Who was affected?
4. Was the change temporary or permanent?
5. Did the user later reverse it?
6. Which project/version did it belong to?

## Purpose
The map exists to prevent the archive from becoming a list of characters. The user's worldbuilding is most valuable when relationships produce consequences.
