# Almont — Reign Profile / Recovery Matrix

## Current high-confidence state
Almont is remembered as Emperor and tyrannical ruler of a vast imperial system. He is husband of Arisa, father of Ryota in the strongest family branch, and associated with Benjamin as adviser.

## Two Almonts to recover
The archive must distinguish:

### Almont as institution
- emperor;
- source of centralized authority;
- political center of the imperial system;
- owner/issuer of military and administrative power.

### Almont as person
- husband;
- father;
- individual with motives, fears, attachments, resentments, expectations.

A scene can move between these identities without announcing the transition. Future extraction should record which identity is operating at each moment.

## Reign evidence to recover
- how Almont gives orders;
- how punishment works;
- how dissent is handled;
- how military cities report upward;
- how succession is managed;
- how he reacts when a politically important family member resists;
- how court officials affect him;
- how he treats Arisa privately versus publicly;
- whether the empire depends on his personal presence or on institutions.

## Psychological questions
- What does Almont believe a ruler owes his empire?
- Does he believe a father and emperor can be separated?
- Does he confuse obedience with love?
- Does he interpret Ryota's departures as betrayal, weakness, strategy, or something else?
- Does he ever fear losing control of Ryota rather than simply losing an heir?

These remain recovery targets.

## Scale consistency
The remembered empire is so large that Almont's personal psychology cannot be the only explanation for its continuity. The archive should recover which institutions make his reign operational.

## Canon protection
Do not retroactively make Almont sympathetic or purely monstrous. The existing memory only fixes his tyranny and harsh family relationship at a broad level; scene evidence should provide the interior complexity.
