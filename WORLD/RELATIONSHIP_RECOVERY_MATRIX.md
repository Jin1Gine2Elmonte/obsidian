# Relationship Recovery Matrix

## Purpose
Character relationships are treated as dynamic systems rather than static labels. The current durable context is incomplete, so this file records the high-value relationships to recover from historical conversations.

## Jin ↔ Almont
### Confirmed
The accessible memory contains an imperial/child-history branch involving Almont and material about Jin/Ryota, but it is not sufficiently detailed to establish a single exact Jin–Almont relationship across all versions.

### Recovery priorities
- Is the Jin in the Almont branch a specific version?
- What did Almont know about Jin?
- Was their relationship familial, political, adversarial, or something else?
- Did either understand the other's larger identity?
- Which scenes define the relationship?

## Jin ↔ Ryota
### Confirmed nearby material
Ryota is Almont's son and has a traumatic history involving Arisa's death, rebellion, departures, and return.

### Recovery priorities
- Why is Jin attached to this branch?
- Are Jin and Ryota distinct identities or does the historical material use Jin as an alternate name/version?
- Does one become or precede the other?
- What memories overlap?
- What is different in personality and narrative function?

This is a high-priority ambiguity because resolving it can restructure the entire Almont-family branch.

## Jin ↔ Arisa
The durable context establishes Arisa as Almont's wife and preserves her death as a major event witnessed in the child-history branch. The nature of her direct relationship with Jin remains under-recovered.

## Jin ↔ Adam
Adam is a traveler between worlds and follower of Jin. The most important missing information is the mechanism and emotional reason behind that following.

## Almont ↔ Arisa
They are husband and wife in the remembered imperial branch. Historical scenes are required to reconstruct affection, conflict, ideology, dependence, and political partnership without reducing them to generic roles.

## Almont ↔ Ryota
The relationship is characterized in durable memory by authority, rebellion, estrangement, and lack of conventional familial warmth. Ryota repeatedly leaves/disappears and later returns because his position and future influence matter.

## Almont ↔ Benjamin
Benjamin is an adviser. Exact influence, loyalty, ideology, and private relationship remain under-recovered.

## Garthin ↔ others
Exact relationships to Jin, Almont, Adam, and the imperial branch are not currently confirmed by accessible memory. Do not create direct plot links merely because the characters inhabit related dark-fantasy material.

## Relationship state fields
For every recovered pair, record:
- relationship type;
- start point;
- turning points;
- current state;
- historical states;
- asymmetry of knowledge;
- emotional force;
- power imbalance;
- shared secrets;
- explicit conflict;
- consequence;
- source evidence.

## Key principle
A relationship should explain change. If recording the relationship does not tell a future reader why either character behaves differently because of the other, the record is incomplete.
