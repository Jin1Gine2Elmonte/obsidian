# Garthin — Scene Recovery Targets

## Objective
Recover the actual character through moments in which his public identity and private impulse collide.

## Priority scene classes
1. First public demonstration of authority.
2. First consequential act of brutality.
3. First moment in which he notices vulnerability.
4. First hidden act of care.
5. First contradiction observed by another character.
6. A decision where cruelty would be easier than restraint.
7. A moment where restraint causes a political cost.
8. A private moment immediately after violence.
9. A scene involving someone who is not useful to him.
10. A scene where his hidden kindness is misunderstood.

## Extraction fields
For every recovered scene:
- setting;
- participants;
- public behavior;
- private behavior;
- stated motive;
- implied motive;
- action refused;
- action chosen;
- witness reaction;
- consequence;
- later callback;
- whether the scene changes the character model.

## Anti-simplification rule
Never summarize Garthin as simply "evil but secretly good." The useful structure is unresolved contradiction. A tender act can coexist with cruelty without redeeming it; cruelty can coexist with attachment without explaining it away.

## Recovery question
The highest-value question is not "is Garthin good?" It is "what does Garthin do that his own public identity makes difficult to explain?"
