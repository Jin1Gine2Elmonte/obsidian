# Dark Forest — Boundary of the Empire

## Core memory
The Dark Forest is one of the most important geographic/world-system anchors. It is dangerous, mysterious, and not controlled by the empire.

## Structural function
The Dark Forest is more than a dangerous place. It marks the point where imperial certainty and centralized authority stop being complete. It therefore creates a counterforce to an empire that otherwise controls most of the world.

## Three layers of importance
### Geographic
It is a physical region that lies outside effective imperial control.

### Political
Its existence limits the empire's claim to total authority.

### Epistemic
It represents territory whose rules, inhabitants, history, and dangers are not fully absorbed into imperial knowledge.

## Relationship to Almont
If Almont's empire is understood as centralized order, the Dark Forest is the major external remainder that resists totalization. Exact political policy toward it is not fully recovered.
