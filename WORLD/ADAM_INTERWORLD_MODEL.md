# Adam — Interworld Model

## Confirmed input
Adam travels between worlds and follows Jin.

## Structural possibilities to test
World travel can function in several fundamentally different ways:
- ability possessed by Adam;
- access granted by Jin;
- property of the world's cosmology;
- consequence of being connected to Jin;
- a learned technique;
- an artifact/system.

No option is currently canonical.

## Questions with high explanatory value
1. What does Adam experience when crossing worlds?
2. Does every world obey the same laws?
3. Does Adam retain memories across worlds?
4. Can Adam bring objects/people across boundaries?
5. What determines destination?
6. Can a world reject him?
7. What does crossing cost?
8. Does Adam know when he has entered a different version of reality?
9. Does Adam function as a bridge for the reader between unrelated stories?
10. Does Jin control the destination or merely understand the network?

## Character consequence
If Adam can travel independently, his agency is structurally stronger. If he can travel only because of Jin, his relationship to Jin becomes an ontological dependency. Historical scenes should resolve which kind of relationship the story actually uses.

## Recovery priority
Search conversations containing Adam together with world, travel, portal, crossing, return, Jin, version, memory, or destination language. Preserve the surrounding scene, because isolated keyword matches cannot determine the mechanism.
