## Recovery method
Whenever a transcript becomes available, do not search only by project title. Search by names, locations, distinctive phrases, actions, and relationship pairs. The same story may be discussed without repeating its title.

## Resolution rule
When a gap is answered:
1. update the smallest relevant node;
2. update relationship edges;
3. update timeline;
4. update canon ledger;
5. update any high-density synthesis that depended on the old state;
6. preserve obsolete statements as historical evidence when they explain the evolution.
