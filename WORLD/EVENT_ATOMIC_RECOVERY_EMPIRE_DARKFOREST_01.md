# Atomic World/Event Recovery — Empire & Dark Forest

## Status
Partial atomic recovery from currently accessible memory. This is an evidence-bounded event/state layer, not reconstructed canon.

## Event / State 01 — Imperial near-global control
- **Recoverable core:** The empire is remembered as controlling essentially the known world outside the Dark Forest.
- **Type:** macro-world state.
- **Evidence strength:** high within durable context.
- **Consequences that are structurally implied:** enormous administrative, military, communication, resource, and succession demands.
- **Important boundary:** those institutional details are implications, not automatically recovered canon.

## Event / State 02 — 1,060 military cities
- **Recoverable core:** Earlier worldbuilding established approximately 1,060 military cities.
- **Type:** established scale/worldbuilding state.
- **Evidence strength:** high within durable context.
- **Open questions:** numbering purpose, hierarchy, geography, governors, military functions, population stability, and inter-city coordination.

## Event / State 03 — Extraordinary city scale
- **Recoverable core:** Some imagined individual cities reached populations in the hundreds of millions.
- **Type:** declared scale detail.
- **Evidence strength:** high within durable context.
- **Boundary:** Do not derive real-world demographic or logistical calculations and promote them to canon unless source material explicitly supports them.

## Event / State 04 — Dark Forest as imperial limit
- **Recoverable core:** The Dark Forest is outside normal imperial control.
- **Type:** geographic/political/epistemic boundary state.
- **Evidence strength:** high within durable context.
- **What is not known:** exact reason, inhabitants, factions, physical or supernatural properties, expedition history, relationship to the power system, and cosmological secrets.

## Event / State 05 — Empire is vast but not total
- **Recoverable core:** The empire's near-global reach coexists with a region that defeats assumptions of complete centralized control.
- **Type:** structural world contradiction.
- **Analytical value:** preserves a meaningful limit on imperial power without inventing its cause.

## Event / State 06 — Character departures intersect imperial geography
- **Recoverable core:** Repeated departure from the capital by the imperial child/young figure should be understood against the empire's geography and political reach.
- **Type:** relationship between character event and world state.
- **Boundary:** The available memory establishes the departure/return pattern but does not establish that any specific departure entered the Dark Forest.

## Event / State 07 — Institutional consequences are unresolved
The declared imperial scale implies the existence of mechanisms for command, logistics, taxation, communication, records, transport, succession, and governance. These remain **derived recovery targets**, not canonical events.

## Recovery targets
1. Specific scenes showing the empire's scale rather than describing it.
2. Named political/military events involving Almont.
3. Any concrete rebellion, suppression, succession crisis, or provincial conflict.
4. The first known imperial encounter with the Dark Forest, if such an event exists.
5. Expedition/war/negotiation history concerning the Forest.
6. How characters' movements intersect with imperial control.
7. Evidence for whether the 1,060-city figure changed across project generations.

## Preservation rule
Never turn a structural implication into an event. A state can be recorded as established even when the causal history producing it remains unknown.
