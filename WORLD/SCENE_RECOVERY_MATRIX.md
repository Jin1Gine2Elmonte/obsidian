# Scene Recovery Matrix

The richest historical memory is often embedded in scenes rather than summaries. This matrix defines what to recover from old conversations once transcripts are available.

## Scene as an event-transition unit
A recovered scene should not be stored only as description. Treat it as a transition unit:

`preceding state -> pressure/trigger -> action -> immediate consequence -> new state`

Then record what changed in:
- identity;
- relationships;
- knowledge;
- world state;
- project canon;
- future decisions.

This allows one recovered scene to feed the timeline, causal map, relationship state, version lattice, canon ledger, and knowledge-event history without copying the scene into each file.

## Jin scenes
Recover:
- first appearance in each version;
- first explicit demonstration of awareness;
- first reality-altering act;
- moments of restraint;
- moments of emotional vulnerability;
- interactions with different versions;
- scenes where another character misunderstands Jin;
- scenes where Jin's power creates an unintended consequence;
- scenes that explicitly connect or separate Jin versions.

## Almont scenes
Recover:
- first appearance as emperor;
- private behavior away from court;
- interactions with Arisa;
- interactions with the child/son and any name used for him in that version;
- conversations with Benjamin;
- decisions showing his worldview;
- reactions to rebellion;
- reaction to Arisa's death;
- response to departures and returns.

## Ryota / child-history scenes
Recover:
- childhood before the traumatic rupture;
- the scene involving Arisa's death;
- immediate aftermath;
- first act of rebellion;
- first departure from the capital;
- life outside the capital;
- reason for return;
- conflict with Almont after returning;
- scenes where political duty conflicts with personal freedom;
- any explicit evidence connecting or separating the child from Jin.

## Garthin scenes
Recover:
- first establishment of his authority;
- first brutal act;
- first contradiction;
- first unmistakable act of hidden kindness;
- who notices it;
- whether Garthin notices himself doing it;
- scenes where his public authority collides with private feeling.

## Adam scenes
Recover:
- origin or first encounter with Jin;
- first world crossing;
- explanation of his following;
- independent action;
- disagreement with Jin;
- discovery about another world;
- discovery about Jin;
- consequence of choosing to remain with Jin;
- any scene showing whether Adam recognizes different Jin variants.

## World scenes
Recover:
- first depiction of the empire's scale;
- first numbered military city;
- capital structure;
- Dark Forest boundary;
- power-system explanation;
- blood-inheritance event;
- spiritual-vessel event;
- any event demonstrating the difference between Magic and Powers.

## Extraction fields
Every recovered scene should receive:
- source/conversation date;
- project/version;
- location;
- participants;
- preceding state;
- pressure/trigger;
- immediate action;
- immediate consequence;
- resulting state;
- revelation;
- information deliberately hidden;
- canon change;
- relationship change;
- identity/version impact;
- causal impact;
- uncertainty;
- links to related scenes;
- superseded interpretation if applicable.

## Scene priority
Prioritize scenes by **downstream change**, not by dramatic intensity:
1. scenes that establish or resolve identity/version boundaries;
2. scenes that permanently change relationships;
3. scenes that trigger major world-state changes;
4. scenes that establish rules of powers/world mechanics;
5. scenes that explain later decisions;
6. scenes that add characterization without changing larger structure.

Scenes that changed later decisions have higher memory value than scenes that merely repeat existing descriptions.

## Anti-reconstruction rule
Do not fill an unrecovered scene from the surrounding summary. Preserve the missing scene as a recovery target while allowing only independently supported facts to survive outside it.
