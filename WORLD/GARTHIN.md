# Garthin — Contradictory Ruler Anchor

## Core memory
Garthin is remembered as a ruler who can be brutal and monstrous while carrying an obscure internal spark of kindness. The important quality is not redemption; it is contradiction.

## Character principle
The kindness should not be delivered as a speech explaining that Garthin "really has a good heart." It should leak through:
- involuntary reactions;
- strange restraint;
- behavior toward vulnerable people;
- moments where cruelty and care appear simultaneously;
- timing that reveals a hidden priority;
- actions he refuses to explain.

## Why the contradiction matters
The user's preferred characterization rejects simplistic good/evil moral labeling. Garthin therefore represents a character whose actions can be terrible while his internal motives remain complex, fragmented, or partially inaccessible even to himself.

This makes Garthin useful as a test of the broader creative philosophy: **meaning should be discovered through behavior, not announced by the narrator.**

## What is actually known
- He is a ruler.
- He can be violent/brutal.
- He contains a hidden or difficult-to-explain kindness.
- That kindness becomes visible most strongly under intense circumstances.
- He is meant to feel psychologically layered rather than morally binary.

## What is not sufficiently recovered
- exact kingdom/territory;
- chronology;
- age;
- origin;
- political relationship to Almont/empire;
- exact powers;
- family;
- defining trauma;
- major scenes;
- ending/fate;
- whether he is in the same universe generation as every other Garthin mention.

## Recovery priorities
Search future conversation material for scenes rather than only descriptions. Garthin's real character is likely encoded in actions: who he spares, who he punishes, what he notices, what he hides, and what he does when no political benefit exists.

## Canon protection
Do not turn the phrase "hidden kindness" into a generic redemption arc. The durable memory does not establish that Garthin becomes good. It establishes a contradiction that must remain alive unless the user later changes it.
