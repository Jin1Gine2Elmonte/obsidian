# Imperial Institutional Model

## Purpose
Translate remembered imperial scale into a structured set of questions and constraints without inventing missing canon.

## Established inputs
- Almont is emperor.
- The empire controls most of the world.
- The Dark Forest remains outside effective imperial control.
- The setting includes approximately 1,060 military cities.
- Some cities have extraordinarily large populations within the fictional scale.

## Institutional dimensions to recover
### Command
- How authority flows from Almont to cities.
- Whether cities are directly ruled or locally administered.
- How military and civilian authority overlap.

### Communication
- How orders travel across the empire.
- Whether magical or unusual communication exists.
- What delay means for rebellion, war, and emergency decisions.

### Logistics
- Food.
- Weapons.
- Recruitment.
- Roads/ports/routes.
- Storage.
- Medical support.
- Movement of troops.

### Governance
- Taxes.
- Local officials.
- Law.
- Courts.
- Enforcement.
- Intelligence.
- Noble/familial influence.

### Succession
Almont's family structure makes succession a likely institutional pressure point. Historical material must determine the actual succession rules rather than assuming a generic hereditary monarchy.

## Capital as mechanism
The capital should be treated as more than a location. In the remembered character history, it functions as a place characters can leave and return to while remaining politically important. This suggests an intersection between geographic location and institutional gravity.

## Dark Forest as boundary
The Dark Forest can be modeled as an explicit exception to imperial totality. The useful question is what kind of failure creates the boundary: geography, military danger, information failure, supernatural interference, cultural autonomy, or another mechanism.

## Canon boundary
This document contains analytical reconstruction targets, not a completed imperial constitution. Exact institutions require source recovery or explicit future canon decisions.
