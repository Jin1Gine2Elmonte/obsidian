# Questions & Open Loops

A strong memory system should preserve not only answers but unanswered questions. Open questions prevent future AI sessions from treating unfinished thinking as finished canon.

## Fiction / cosmology
- What is the exact ontological hierarchy connecting Existence Sublime, Jin, the Primordial Rift, and the Entity of Forgetting?
- What precisely happened in the First Creation, and what are the Shadows: beings, memories, laws, remnants, or consequences?
- What is the exact mechanism by which the imagination realm influences ordinary humans?
- What are the limits, costs, and failure modes of Jin's reality control?
- How does the Entity of Forgetting actually operate without collapsing the philosophical distinction between nothingness and existence?
- What does the Primordial Rift do to causality, memory, or reality?

## Power system
- What are the exact rules separating Magic from Powers?
- What can a spiritual vessel store, channel, transform, or destroy?
- What are the costs and failure modes of inherited Powers?
- Can blood inheritance be altered, transferred, suppressed, or falsified?

## Empire / world
- How does an empire with ~1,060 military cities coordinate communication, logistics, taxation, and military command?
- What is the exact geography of the empire and the Dark Forest?
- Why has the empire failed to control the Dark Forest?
- What institutions exist beneath the emperor?
- How do ordinary people experience the empire's enormous scale?

## Character psychology
- What exact internal wound drives each major character?
- Which behaviors are deliberate and which are involuntary?
- What does Almont believe he is protecting when he exercises control?
- What does Ryota actually want beneath rebellion?
- What private pattern reveals Garthin's hidden kindness without redeeming him?

## AI memory
- What is the minimum viable Obsidian + MCP architecture?
- Which component should be local and which, if any, should be remote?
- How should semantic retrieval be combined with direct links and full-text search?
- How should conversation imports be normalized without destroying the original transcript?
- How should model-specific memories and model-independent memory be separated?
- How should stale memories be detected and superseded?

## Nexus / agents
- Which Nexus iteration is the canonical architecture, if any?
- Which components are experiments and which are production-worthy?
- Where should memory live relative to the agent runtime?
- What is the smallest architecture that preserves the desired autonomy?

## Writing craft
- Where does mystery currently exceed clarity?
- Which recurring strengths should become deliberate techniques rather than instinctive habits?
- Which weaknesses remain undertrained: pacing, scene transitions, dialogue, exposition, structure, or revision discipline?

## Archive questions
- Which historical conversations should be imported verbatim next?
- Which memories deserve independent notes rather than inclusion in broad summaries?
- Which concepts have grown large enough to become anchors?
