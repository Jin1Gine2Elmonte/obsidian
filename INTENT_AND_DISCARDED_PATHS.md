# Intent, Revisions, and Discarded Paths

## Purpose
Preserve not only what exists now, but what a project was trying to become and which directions were later abandoned or superseded.

## Why this matters
A final summary hides the creative struggle that produced it. That hidden history can contain constraints, reasons, and negative knowledge: things deliberately rejected, not merely forgotten.

## Writing / fiction
### Mystery and depth
The user repeatedly values mystery, depth, psychological ambiguity, and intimate darkness. Later self-diagnosis identified a risk: mystery can outrun clarity and pacing.

**Preserved lesson:** do not interpret an increase in mystery as automatic improvement.

### Moral simplification
The user rejects simple good/evil compression in favor of characters whose behavior can contain contradictory forces.

**Preserved lesson:** a later scene should not "redeem" a contradictory character merely because the audience discovers kindness.

### Mythology
The user prefers world mythology to arise from internal creation and logic rather than obvious cultural imitation.

**Preserved lesson:** borrow structural inspiration only; preserve original metaphysical identity.

## Jin
A recurring historical risk is converting later cosmic/metafictional power into a universal definition of every earlier Jin.

**Rejected archival path:** one giant canonical Jin profile containing every ability and biography from every generation.

**Preferred path:** version lattice + local canon + cross-version invariants.

## Almont / family branch
A recurring risk is reducing the family branch to "tyrant father / rebellious son." The preserved structure is more specific: institutional authority and family authority overlap, while repeated departure/return creates accumulated history.

**Rejected archival path:** psychological interpretation without scene evidence.

**Preferred path:** state transitions + scene recovery + motive recovery.

## Garthin
The hidden kindness is not a discarded redemption arc. It is a retained contradiction.

**Rejected interpretation:** secretly good ruler.

**Preferred interpretation:** behavior reveals a private force that does not cancel public brutality.

## Dark Forest / Empire
The scale of the empire is preserved rather than reduced for realism. Institutional questions are derived consequences, not retroactive canon.

**Rejected archival path:** inventing a complete medieval bureaucracy and pretending it was remembered.

**Preferred path:** preserve declared scale, record structural consequences as analysis, recover actual institutions from source.

## Strings of Fate
Current remembered material is heavily visual. The archive explicitly rejects filling the missing story with common fate-story mechanics simply because the title invites them.

**Rejected archival path:** automatically introducing red strings, deterministic fate, time travel, or a fate deity.

**Preferred path:** preserve visual canon; recover narrative canon from source.

## Entity of Forgetting
The strongest preserved idea is philosophical rather than mechanical: nothingness cannot manifest as an entity while remaining absolute nothingness.

**Rejected archival path:** turning the Entity into "nothingness with a face."

**Preferred path:** preserve the ontological distinction and recover the actual narrative mechanism later.

## Memory system
The archive itself evolved through correction.

**Early risk:** more files were treated as progress.

**Corrective direction:** update existing files, remove duplication, preserve provenance, represent contradictions, and prioritize high-impact unknowns.

## Negative knowledge principle
A discarded idea is useful when it explains a boundary: why a concept should not be interpreted in a certain way, why two projects must remain separate, or why a previous synthesis was invalid.

The archive should therefore preserve important rejected paths without promoting them to canon.
