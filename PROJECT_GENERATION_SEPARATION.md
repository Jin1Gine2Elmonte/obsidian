# Project Generation Separation

## Purpose
The archive contains projects that may share language, imagery, or concepts but were developed at different times and may not share a single canon.

## Generation markers to preserve
For every recovered project statement, record when possible:
- project
- world/universe
- development phase
- version
- whether it is active, abandoned, superseded, or exploratory
- source conversation/date

## Why this matters for Jin
Jin/Gin/Gene has multiple remembered iterations. A later cosmic/meta-fictional form must not overwrite an earlier human-scale or imperial form. Conversely, an early form must not be forced into the later cosmology merely because the name survived.

## Why this matters for cosmology
Existence Sublime, Entity of Forgetting, Primordial Rift, Shadows of the First Creation, Corruption, Curse of the Ancients, Blood Family, Dreams of the Inverted Universe, and other anchors may belong to different project generations. The current archive confirms their recurrence but not universal co-membership.

## Why this matters for Strings of Fate
The visual project is strongly remembered, while broader narrative data is not equally preserved. Do not import Jin, Garthin, Entity of Forgetting, or other anchors into Strings of Fate without source evidence.

## Status vocabulary
- CURRENT_CANON
- HISTORICAL_CANON
- WORKING_DRAFT
- EXPLORATORY
- ABANDONED
- SUPERSEDED
- UNRESOLVED

## Migration rule
When a project is rewritten:
1. preserve the old state;
2. identify the exact change;
3. mark the old state as superseded only when the change is explicit;
4. update affected relationships;
5. do not silently rewrite history.

## Core principle
A creative project has history. A later idea can replace an earlier one artistically without making the earlier idea false as a historical record of development.
