# I. Fiction / world memory not yet deeply represented

## Jin — broader remembered architecture
- Jin also appears as Gin/Gene in different contexts; alias normalization must remain version-scoped.
- A recurring premise places imagination itself as a realm/place that does not physically exist in ordinary reality but can still influence humans and their reality.
- Jin is associated with awareness that he is fictional and with the ability to manipulate/control reality.
- The important creative tension is not simply power scale: the fictional boundary, authorship, reality, causality, choice, and the meaning of action remain active questions around Jin.
- Jin can function as protagonist, observer, creator-like agent, causal disruptor, cosmological actor, or bridge between worlds depending on project/version.
- The user has repeatedly treated multiple Jin versions as significant enough that their identities, powers, chronology, memories, and relationships must not be flattened.
- The complete genealogy of these versions is not currently available; historical extraction remains required.

## Almont / imperial family
- Almont is remembered as an emperor/sovereign and tyrannical father figure in the imperial-family branch.
- Arisa is his wife.
- Benjamin is an advisor closely associated with Almont's political sphere.
- Ryota is remembered as Almont's son in one durable branch.
- A separate durable memory describes a child/young Jin experiencing an almost identical Arisa-death / escape / return / rebellion history.
- The identity relation between Jin and Ryota remains deliberately unresolved.
- The family branch is not merely a simple tyrant-vs-rebel narrative; fatherhood, sovereignty, inheritance, recognition, political position, emotional distance, and repeated return all overlap.
- The son repeatedly leaves/disappears into other lands and later returns because his position/future influence makes the capital politically consequential.
- Returning does not equal reconciliation.
- The remembered relationship is harsh and lacks conventional warmth, but lack of warmth should not be converted into proof that all attachment is absent.

## Arisa / rupture
- Arisa's death is remembered as a pivotal trauma in the child-history branch.
- The child witnesses the death and then escapes/leaves.
- Exact circumstances, agents, preceding family conditions, and Almont's immediate reaction are not currently preserved at scene level.
- Arisa's own characterization and motives before her death remain substantially under-recovered.

## Empire
- The worldbuilding includes an enormous empire controlling most of the world.
- A repeatedly remembered scale is **1,060 military cities**, with exceptionally large population figures in some cities (including remembered figures reaching hundreds of millions).
- The declaration of scale is part of the remembered world model; exact logistics, taxation, communications, political subdivisions, and institutional mechanisms should not be invented merely to rationalize the scale.
- The capital is politically central because the son cannot permanently erase the significance of his position there.

## Dark Forest
- A dangerous and mysterious region remains outside effective imperial control.
- The Dark Forest is therefore not merely a location but a structural limit on imperial totality.
- The exact reason for the boundary (military, geographical, epistemic, supernatural, or mixed) remains under-recovered.

## Power system
- Two broad power categories are remembered:
  - **Magic** — more transcendent, beyond ordinary logic, and difficult to learn.
  - **Powers** — comparatively logical/limited abilities associated with inheritance through blood.
- A **spiritual vessel** concept exists within the power system.
- The political implications of inherited Powers, bloodline, succession, marriage, military value, and family identity are strong analytical consequences, but detailed mechanics require source recovery.

## Garthin
- Garthin is a ruler characterized by brutality combined with a hidden spark of kindness.
- The contradiction is meant to appear through behavior rather than exposition.
- The user explicitly prefers that this contradiction not collapse into a conventional 'secretly good ruler' redemption.
- Garthin's inner kindness is better understood as a persistent internal force that coexists with public brutality.
- Exact scenes, history, relationships, and causes of this contradiction remain under-recovered.

## Adam
- Adam is remembered as a traveler between worlds and a follower/companion of Jin.
- His interworld movement may make him an important observational anchor for distinguishing Jin variants.
- The archive does not yet possess the first meeting, complete motivation, independent goals, or detailed travel mechanics.
