# Fiction Canon — Deep Record

> **Role:** deep cross-domain fiction synthesis. This file preserves a high-density historical/working synthesis across fiction, cosmology, world systems, and character architecture; it is **not** a competing canonical owner. Actual canon remains with the specialized domain owners and `CANON_AND_PROVENANCE.md`.

## Foundational metaphysics
The major fictional body of work is oriented around existence, imagination, fictionality, memory, forgetting, reality, and the instability of conventional boundaries between them.

A recurring design principle is that metaphysical ideas must behave like real forces inside the setting. A cosmological concept should not exist only as a title; it should alter perception, causality, institutions, characters, history, or the rules of reality.

## Imagination as a realm
One major premise treats imagination as a domain that does not physically exist in ordinary reality yet can influence human beings. This allows the story to question whether influence requires material existence and whether a fictional entity can possess agency within a layered reality.

Jin/Gin/Gene occupies a special position in this architecture because awareness of fictional existence and reality control are related aspects of the same problem: what happens when an entity understands the boundary between narrative representation and the world it inhabits?

## Existence Sublime
"Existence Sublime" / الوجود الأبهى is a high-level cosmological anchor. Treat it as a concept with consequences, not merely a superlative title. Its full canonical mechanics are not exhaustively established in the available record; therefore future details should be marked as working or speculative until explicitly confirmed.

## Entity of Forgetting
The key paradox is foundational:
- Absolute nothingness cannot appear as an entity.
- Appearance is already a form of existence.
- Therefore an entity that represents or performs forgetting must be something other than literal nothingness.

This creates a rich distinction between **nothingness** and a **function that removes, obscures, transforms, or destroys memory/meaning**.

The concept can therefore participate simultaneously in ontology, information theory, narrative identity, and emotional loss without collapsing them into the same thing.

## Primordial Rift
The Primordial Rift is a recurring cosmological anchor connected to the deepest history of the fictional reality. Exact mechanics remain under development and should be extended only when supported by later canon.

## Shadows of the First Creation
A central mythological title/anchor concerned with origins and the consequences of the first creation. It is connected to primordial cosmology, Existence Sublime, Entity of Forgetting, and the structure of reality.

## Moral architecture
The universe should not rely on a simple moral binary. Good and evil can be treated as weak abstractions compared with forces such as desire, fear, domination, grief, repression, loyalty, hunger, memory, identity, and survival.

This does not mean characters lack ethical consequences. It means the narrative should resist reducing complex causality to moral labels.

## Garthin
Garthin is a central experiment in contradictory characterization. His violence is not canceled by kindness, and kindness does not redeem violence automatically. The character becomes interesting because both impulses can be real simultaneously.

The narrative should prefer evidence such as:
- what he does when no one is watching
- what he refuses to say
- moments where instinct contradicts doctrine
- small protective gestures that cost him something
- sudden emotional absences or hesitations
- the mismatch between public identity and private reaction

## Almont family axis
Almont is a tyrannical imperial center. Arisa, Benjamin, and Ryota form a family/political structure where personal history and imperial power overlap.

Ryota's relationship to Almont is built around rebellion, emotional harshness, and lack of conventional warmth. Arisa's death is a major traumatic event in the history and is connected to later escape and return.

The important structural principle is that family conflict should not be treated as a side plot separated from politics; the empire itself should amplify the family's psychological dynamics.

## Empire scale
The world has included approximately 1,060 military cities and extraordinary population scales. Such magnitude implies consequences for logistics, communications, bureaucracy, military doctrine, trade, food systems, internal migration, and regional identity. Future expansion should respect those implications rather than using the scale only as spectacle.

## Dark Forest
The Dark Forest is dangerous, mysterious, and outside effective imperial control. It functions as both geographic boundary and conceptual counterweight to imperial order. Its narrative value increases when the forest exposes truths, behaviors, or forces that imperial systems cannot fully classify.

## Power system
Magic is transcendent and difficult to learn; Powers are comparatively logical, constrained, and often inherited through blood. A spiritual-vessel concept exists.

A strong future design direction is to ensure that the two categories differ not merely in power level but in ontology, learning curve, cost, social consequences, and the kinds of questions each system allows characters to ask.

## Narrative rule
Cosmological ideas should eventually have local consequences. If a metaphysical concept is true at the highest level but never changes a character's choices, institutions, language, or sensory experience, it is probably functioning as decoration rather than living worldbuilding.
