## Directly remembered with high confidence
The project is named **أوتار القدر / Strings of Fate**. The strongest surviving memory concerns a cover/visual revision in which:
- strings are translucent or semi-transparent white;
- their glow carries a slight pale-emerald/green tint rather than becoming plainly green;
- the sky color must remain unchanged;
- the city, character, lighting, main color relationships, composition, title/font identity, and dimensions should remain intact unless explicitly requested otherwise;
- unwanted bottom writing should be removed;
- decoration below the title should be improved;
- the result should avoid generic AI-looking artifacts.

These are evidence-level memories and are therefore preserved here, but canonical visual ownership remains with the dedicated visual files.

## Interpretation boundary
The visual requests support an artistic preference for controlled local change, integration with existing light/depth, restraint, and preservation of original identity. These are interpretations, not plot mechanics.

## Major missing evidence
The accessible memory does not currently provide a complete source-level record for:
- protagonist and complete cast;
- plot chronology;
- chapter structure;
- setting details;
- origin or mechanics of the strings;
- who can perceive or manipulate them;
- relationship to fate, choice, time, memory, death, love, probability, or causality;
- exact ending;
- canonical relationship to Jin or the broader cosmological anchors.

These are recovery gaps, not invitations to invent.

## Unsafe assumptions
The following must not be promoted to canon without source evidence:
- red-string mythology;
- a conscious fate entity;
- deterministic destiny;
- time travel;
- a fixed string-cutting mechanic;
- a fate deity;
- a direct Jin connection;
- a specific free-will-versus-destiny plot.
