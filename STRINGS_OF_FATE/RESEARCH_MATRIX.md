## Design principle

No single domain should be filled merely to make the archive look complete. Unknown is a legitimate state. The project becomes stronger when missing information remains visible and can later be resolved by actual creative decisions.

## High-value future questions

1. What makes this incarnation of fate different from every familiar "red thread" or destiny trope?
2. What can the reader understand early without destroying the deeper mystery?
3. What is the emotional cost of seeing a relationship as a thread rather than as an ordinary human bond?
4. What does a character do when the string points to an outcome they cannot emotionally accept?
5. Can the system create tragedy without relying on arbitrary prophecy?
6. How can the strings influence plot while remaining more than a decorative gimmick?
7. What visual rule makes the strings unmistakably belong to this project?
8. What single image or scene would communicate the entire philosophy of the project without explanation?
