# أوتار القدر — Canon Registry

## Status
This is a canon registry, not a brainstorming page. Every entry should be labeled by epistemic state.

### State labels
- **CANON** — explicitly established in source material or user-confirmed.
- **WORKING** — current working model used for development but not permanently locked.
- **INFERRED** — logical interpretation derived from established material.
- **SPECULATIVE** — hypothesis or possible direction.
- **UNKNOWN** — deliberately unresolved.
- **SUPERSEDED** — previously valid working state replaced by a newer one.

## Confirmed project anchors

### Title
**Arabic:** أوتار القدر
**English:** Strings of Fate

### Core visual metaphor
The "strings" are a central visual identity. Their presentation is ethereal, luminous, semi-transparent, and physically integrated into the composition rather than appearing as opaque graphic overlays.

### Established cover constraints
The strings should appear white and luminous, with a slight light-emerald tint in their glow. The sky must retain its own color. The existing scene composition, city, character, lighting, color relationships, title/font, and dimensions should remain intact unless explicitly changed. Bottom text that does not belong to the intended final cover should be removed, and the decoration beneath the title can be refined.

### Artistic quality constraint
The result must avoid the visual tells of generic AI output. Texture, light behavior, depth, spatial integration, typography, and restraint matter.

## Deliberately unknown at present

The following are not to be silently invented:
- exact plot chronology
- complete cast list
- exact metaphysics of fate
- literal mechanics of the strings
- exact origin of the strings
- whether strings are physically observable in-world
- who can perceive/manipulate them
- whether fate is deterministic, probabilistic, recursive, self-correcting, or relational
- exact relationship between fate and free will
- exact ending
- complete symbolism dictionary
- definitive cover typography specification beyond established preservation constraints

## Canon rule
When new information arrives, update the smallest relevant node rather than rewriting unrelated parts of the project. Preserve superseded states when they explain development history.
