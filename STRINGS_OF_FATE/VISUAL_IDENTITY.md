# أوتار القدر — Visual Identity System

## Visual thesis
The image language should make the strings feel like a native property of the scene rather than an added special effect. They should have volume, depth, translucency, subtle emission, and a believable relationship with foreground, middle ground, atmosphere, and sky.

## Strings

### Base appearance
- Semi-transparent white.
- Fine luminous structure.
- Slightly soft edges at the brightest regions.
- Subtle light-emerald bias in emission only.
- No heavy green cast.
- No opaque ribbon appearance.
- No plastic or neon-tube look.
- No excessive bloom that destroys local detail.

### Light behavior
The strings should appear to emit or reflect controlled light without flattening the scene. Their illumination should be strongest where narrative emphasis benefits from it and restrained elsewhere. They must not wash the sky or repaint the environment.

### Spatial integration
The strings should obey perspective and depth. Where the scene establishes occlusion, the strings should participate in the same spatial hierarchy instead of floating as a front-layer graphic. Their transparency should allow the existing world to remain visible through them.

### Density
Avoid uniform distribution. Variation in spacing, thickness, luminosity, intersection, and depth should create organic visual rhythm. The system should feel composed rather than procedurally repeated.

## Palette relationship
The emerald note is an accent, not the dominant palette. White remains the perceived base identity of the strings. The environment's established color palette must survive unchanged unless a future explicit decision supersedes it.

## Composition preservation
The strings are allowed to strengthen the existing composition but not casually relocate the focal point. The character, city, sky, title, and major lighting relationships remain the compositional anchors unless later canon changes them.

## Typography
The established title treatment should remain stable unless a deliberate redesign is recorded. Decoration beneath the title can be refined, but refinement must support the title rather than compete with it.

## Anti-generic-AI criteria
Reject variants showing:
- repetitive string patterns
- arbitrary glowing outlines
- uniform neon intensity
- inconsistent perspective
- detached overlays
- excessive bloom
- color contamination
- synthetic symmetry without compositional purpose
- over-sharpened texture
- generic cinematic grading that changes the established image identity

## Asset philosophy
Every major visual revision should preserve the source image identity unless the task explicitly calls for a redesign. Keep a record of which element changed and why.
