# Project & Idea Timeline

This is a reconstructed high-level timeline from currently available context. It is **not** a verbatim transcript timeline. Dates are only used when directly available; otherwise ordering is approximate.

## 1. Early fiction development
- Developed dark fantasy and cosmological fiction centered on Jin/Gin/Gene and a reality/imagination premise.
- Built major empire/family material around Almont, Arisa, Benjamin, Ryota, and the Dark Forest.
- Developed a distinction between Magic and inherited Powers and introduced the spiritual-vessel concept.
- Developed recurring cosmological anchors such as Abyss, Corruption, Curse of the Ancients, Existence Sublime, Entity of Forgetting, Primordial Rift, and Shadows of the First Creation.

## 2. Novel architecture phase
- Developed first sections of a major novel, reaching at least section 9 in one stage.
- Explored a 20-chapter main-novel structure.
- Developed Whispers of Fog as a separate medieval-fantasy project with a 20-chapter concept.

## 3. Craft-awareness phase
- Recognized that mystery and depth were improving faster than clarity and pacing.
- Identified writing gaps as a structural problem rather than something solved simply by adding more lore or mystery.
- Continued refining the balance among mystery, clarity, emotional movement, narrative logic, and scene purpose.

## 4. Cosmology expansion phase
- Expanded the mythology vocabulary around Existence Sublime, Entity of Forgetting, Primordial Rift, Shadows of the First Creation, Corruption, Curse of the Ancients, Blood family, and Dreams of the Inverted Universe.
- Developed the philosophical distinction that an entity associated with forgetting is not identical to absolute nothingness, because manifestation itself makes it existent.
- Began treating recurring concepts as possible anchor nodes while preserving uncertainty about whether they belong to one canonical universe.

## 5. AI tooling and agent phase
- Explored Gemini, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, GLM, Nemotron, image/video/audio systems, and agentic workflows.
- Developed multiple Nexus concepts involving servers, agent orchestration, tools, and persistent memory.
- Worked with Hermes CLI/YAML and deployment/knowledge-storage experiments.

## 6. Memory architecture phase
- Investigated cross-model memory and ways to preserve conversation context outside any single model.
- Explored conversation organization, semantic search, provenance, project/character extraction, and persistent storage.
- Explored Obsidian as a durable Markdown source of truth.
- Explored MCP/API/plugin/synchronization bridges and Android interaction-layer concepts.

## 7. GitHub archive phase
- Created `Jin1Gine2Elmonte/obsidian` as a dedicated repository for durable external knowledge.
- Initial repository state contained a minimal README before the archive expanded.
- Expanded the repository into structured memory, fiction, technology, projects, reasoning, relationships, provenance, protocols, and recovery maps.

## 8. World-reconstruction phase
- Built a dedicated `WORLD/` layer for Jin, Jin variants, Almont, Ryota, Arisa, Benjamin, Garthin, Adam, the empire, Dark Forest, and the power system.
- Discovered and preserved the high-impact **Jin ↔ Ryota identity conflict** rather than silently merging the two histories.
- Added causal maps, state machines, transition maps, relationship recovery matrices, motive recovery, and scene-evidence rules.
- Introduced a version lattice so different Jin iterations can branch, rewrite, supersede, or remain unresolved without forced unification.

## 9. Cosmology reconstruction phase
- Added separate memory-density and recovery layers for the major cosmological anchors.
- Explicitly separated recurring thematic relationships from confirmed canonical relationships.
- Identified the lack of a sufficiently sourced global cosmological hierarchy as a genuine unknown rather than a gap to fill creatively.

## 10. Strings of Fate recovery phase
- Created a dedicated `STRINGS_OF_FATE/` boundary.
- Preserved the strongest accessible memory: title identity and detailed cover/visual constraints.
- Distinguished direct visual memory from inferred themes and unsupported plot mechanics.
- Explicitly prohibited importing red-string mythology, deterministic fate, time travel, fate deities, or string mechanics without evidence.

## 11. Historical / negative-memory phase
- Added project-state transition tracking so later versions do not erase meaningful earlier states.
- Added negative knowledge so future models remember which interpretations are unsafe or previously unsupported.
- Added cross-project invariant tracking to preserve recurring conceptual fingerprints without falsely joining projects into one universe.

## 12. Current archive direction
The archive is moving from a document collection toward a connected knowledge system with:
- explicit anchors;
- entity and relationship structures;
- provenance;
- temporal state;
- canon/conflict tracking;
- negative knowledge;
- causal transitions;
- memory-density measures;
- source-first recovery;
- separation of raw history from distilled working memory;
- propagation rules for changes.

## Historical integrity rule
A later summary must never erase an earlier project state merely because the later state is cleaner. When a change matters, preserve the old state, record the transition, identify the evidence, and mark what downstream knowledge was affected.
