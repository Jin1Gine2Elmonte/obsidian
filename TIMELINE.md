# Project & Idea Timeline

This is a reconstructed high-level timeline from currently available context. It is not a verbatim transcript timeline.

## Early fiction development
- Developed dark fantasy and cosmological fiction centered on Jin/Gin/Gene and a reality/imagination premise.
- Built major empire/family material around Almont, Arisa, Benjamin, Ryota, and the Dark Forest.
- Developed a distinction between Magic and inherited Powers and introduced the spiritual-vessel concept.
- Developed recurring cosmological anchors such as Abyss, Corruption, Curse of the Ancients, Existence Sublime, Entity of Forgetting, Primordial Rift, and Shadows of the First Creation.

## Novel architecture phase
- Developed first sections of a major novel, reaching at least section 9 in one stage.
- Explored a 20-chapter main-novel structure.
- Developed Whispers of Fog as a separate medieval-fantasy project with a 20-chapter concept.

## Craft-awareness phase
- Recognized that mystery and depth were improving faster than clarity and pacing.
- Identified writing gaps as a structural problem rather than something solved simply by adding more lore or mystery.
- Continued refining the balance among mystery, clarity, emotional movement, narrative logic, and scene purpose.

## AI tooling phase
- Explored Gemini, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, GLM, Nemotron, image/video/audio systems, and agentic workflows.
- Developed multiple Nexus concepts involving servers, agent orchestration, tools, and persistent memory.
- Worked with Hermes CLI/YAML and deployment/knowledge-storage experiments.

## Memory architecture phase
- Investigated cross-model memory and ways to preserve conversation context outside any single model.
- Explored Gemini conversation organization, semantic search, provenance, project/character extraction, and persistent storage.
- Explored Obsidian as the durable Markdown source of truth.
- Explored MCP/API/plugin/synchronization bridges and Android interaction-layer concepts.

## GitHub archive phase
- Created `Jin1Gine2Elmonte/obsidian` as a dedicated repository for durable external knowledge.
- Initial repository contained only README.md.
- Gradually expanded the repository into structured memory, fiction, technology, project, reasoning, relationship, and protocol files.

## Current direction
The archive is moving from a document collection toward a connected knowledge system with explicit anchors, relationships, provenance, temporal state, and separation of raw history from distilled working memory.
