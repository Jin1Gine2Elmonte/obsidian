## Craft diagnosis attached to this project history
The recurring production problem is not lack of ideas. The difficulty is converting high-density conceptual/world material into scenes that move, reveal, and emotionally transform the reader without flattening the mystery.

Important revision questions:
- What changes because this scene exists?
- What does the reader understand now that was unavailable before?
- What moves physically, causally, emotionally, or relationally?
- Which information should remain unresolved?
- Is atmosphere producing narrative pressure or replacing it?
- Is the abstract idea producing character behavior and consequences?
